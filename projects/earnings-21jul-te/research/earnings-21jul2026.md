# Q1 FY27 Earnings Day — 21 July 2026 — verified data

~45 companies reported Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) today. All comparisons YoY vs Q1 FY26 (Apr–Jun 2025)
unless noted. Sources: Business Standard, Business Today, Free Press Journal, EquityBulls, IANS.

## Autos (strong)
- **Bajaj Auto**: PAT ₹3,225.63 cr, **+46% YoY** (from ₹2,210 cr); revenue ₹21,688.83 cr, **+65%** (from ₹13,133 cr).
  ⚠️ CAVEAT: figures NOT comparable — scope-of-consolidation change (acquired control of BAIHAG, Nov 2025).
  Optical growth; organic growth much lower.
- **TVS Motor**: PAT ₹1,057.61 cr, **+65% YoY** (from ₹642.86 cr); revenue ₹16,295.52 cr, **+33%**.
  Highest-ever Q1 profit; 2W/3W sales +28% to 16.3 lakh; EV sales +86% to ~1.3 lakh; EPS ₹21.46. Stock +6%.

## Teaching angle
- Quarterly results are read **YoY** (vs the same quarter last year), not QoQ — QoQ is distorted by seasonality.
- A headline % can mislead: Bajaj Auto's +65% revenue is largely a consolidation/accounting change, not organic.
- A falling profit isn't always bad: JSW Infra's dip is a deliberate capex trade-off for future growth.
- Read four things: revenue, net profit, margin, and management guidance.
