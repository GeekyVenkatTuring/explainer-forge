# Business Ideas Under ₹25 Lakh — VOLUME 4 (FRANCHISE-based) — Dossier (verified 2026-08-01)

Fourth in the series. Theme: **proven-brand franchises you can own under ₹25 lakh** —
lower risk than a scratch start because you get a known brand, a tested model, training,
and a supply chain. The trade-off: a franchise fee + ongoing royalty + you follow their
rules. This video teaches how to read a franchise deal, not just the idea.

All figures are **indicative 2025-26 ranges**, vary by city/format/brand. Shown on-screen
as RANGES. **Educational only — NOT financial / investment advice.** Franchise terms
(fee, royalty, deposit, lock-in) change — always read the FDD/agreement + verify with the
brand before signing.

Audience: AP/TS first-time founder, budget under ₹25L. Telugu (Tanglish); brands, ₹, %,
numbers stay Latin.

Franchise-specific lenses (added to the 5): **franchise fee** (one-time), **royalty**
(% of monthly sales, forever), **brand pull** (does the name bring customers?),
**territory** (exclusive area?), **support** (training, supply, marketing), **exit/lock-in**
(can you leave?). Watch for aggressive unaudited claims + no-exit clauses.

Legend: 🟢 low · 🟡 medium · 🔴 high. Note: numbers exclude property/rent (varies wildly).

## The 10 franchise ideas (Volume 4)

### 1. QSR food kiosk franchise (QSR ఫుడ్ కియోస్క్) — momos/burger/street-food 🍔
- **Capex ₹5–15 L**: franchise fee ₹1.5–2L, kiosk + equipment, first stock, deposit.
  (Chaat Adda ₹4–8L; Jumboking/WOW! Momo kiosk formats.)
- **Economics**: margin **40–50%**; monthly revenue ₹3–8L (stable outlet); **ROI
  9–18 months**; royalty applies. QSR market ~23% CAGR.
- **Franchise gives**: brand, recipes, training, supply. **Risk**: 🟡 royalty + location +
  discount-driven competition.

### 2. Ice-cream scooping parlour (ఐస్‌క్రీమ్ పార్లర్) — Amul / Naturals 🍦
- **Capex ₹2–10 L**: Amul kiosk from ₹2L, scooping parlour ₹5–10L; deep freezer,
  interiors, stock. **Amul = 0% royalty, no franchise fee.**
- **Economics**: scoops/sundaes up to **50% margin** (packs 15–20%); monthly turnover
  ₹3–6L; **payback 6–11 months**, ROI ~30%.
- **Franchise gives**: brand + product supply. **Risk**: 🟡 seasonal, location; win on
  high-footfall spot + add-ons (shakes, sundaes).

### 3. Courier & e-commerce delivery franchise (కొరియర్ · డెలివరీ) — DTDC / Delhivery 📦
- **Capex ₹0.5–5 L**: joining fee ₹0.5–3L + **refundable security deposit ₹3–10L** +
  scanner/computer/space.
- **Economics**: monthly net **₹50k–1.5L** (parcel-volume driven); e-com shipments ~18%
  CAGR, Tier-2/3 = 40%+ of volume.
- **Franchise gives**: network, tracking tech, pickup/delivery routes. **Risk**: 🟡
  volume-dependent, per-parcel margins thin; win on a high-order pincode.

### 4. Jan Aushadhi generic pharmacy (జన్ ఔషధి కేంద్రం) — PMBJP (govt) 💊
- **Capex ₹2.5–5 L**: furniture/racks, medicine stock, computer + software, interiors.
- **Economics**: **20% margin** on generics + **15% monthly incentive (cap ₹15k)** +
  **govt incentive up to ₹5L**; generics 50–90% cheaper than branded → volume + goodwill.
- **Franchise gives**: govt brand, cheap supply, social trust. **Risk**: 🟡 volume game,
  location near hospitals/clinics wins. Strong social + govt-backed idea.

### 5. Dairy parlour franchise (డెయిరీ పార్లర్) — Amul / Heritage 🥛
- **Capex ₹2–10 L**: deep freezer + visi-cooler, interiors/board, inventory. **0% royalty,
  no fee (Amul).**
- **Economics**: margin **5–20%** (thin, volume game); monthly turnover **₹3–6L**;
  **payback 6–11 months**, ROI ~30%.
- **Franchise gives**: trusted brand + daily-demand products. **Risk**: 🟡 thin margins,
  location + footfall decide it; steady repeat demand.

### 6. Laundry & dry-clean franchise (లాండ్రీ · డ్రై-క్లీన్) — UClean / Tumbledry 🧺
- **Capex ₹15–25 L** (biggest here): franchise fee ₹3L, washers/dryers ₹10L, interiors,
  working capital.
- **Economics**: gross **30–40%**, net 15–20%; monthly profit **₹1.5–2L**; **break-even
  12–18 months**. Laundry market ₹35,000 cr+.
- **Franchise gives**: brand, machines, app, tagging tech. **Risk**: 🔴 high capex +
  **supply lock-in + no-exit clauses** (read the contract!); win on a dense urban catchment.

### 7. Education / skill-coaching franchise (అబాకస్ · స్పోకెన్ ఇంగ్లీష్) — abacus / spoken English 📚
- **Capex ₹0.5–10 L**: abacus from ₹50k (home/hall/school-hours); furniture, kits, small
  space, fee.
- **Economics**: **₹1,000–2,500/child/month**; 50–100 kids → **₹6–30L/year**;
  **break-even 3–5 months** (best unit economics in the list); royalty 10–15%.
- **Franchise gives**: curriculum, teacher training, brand. **Risk**: 🟡 needs enrolment +
  results; word-of-mouth grows it. Coaching industry heading to ₹1.33L cr by 2028.

### 8. Café / coffee franchise (కెఫే · కాఫీ) — branded café/kiosk ☕
- **Capex ₹5–15 L**: franchise fee, espresso machine + equipment, interiors/seating, WC.
- **Economics**: beverage margin **50–60%**, net **15–25%**; ROI 15–24 months; royalty.
- **Franchise gives**: brand, menu, training. **Risk**: 🟡 location + ambience + repeat
  crowd; win on a college/office/mall spot + snacks.

### 9. Supermarket / convenience-store franchise (సూపర్‌మార్కెట్ · కన్వీనియన్స్) 🛒
- **Capex ₹12–25 L**: fee + branding, racks + billing + fridge, **inventory (big WC)**,
  interiors. (Reliance Fresh ₹15–30L; smaller convenience ₹12–18L.)
- **Economics**: margin **8–15%** (thin — a volume game); **payback 18–24 months**.
- **Franchise gives**: brand, supply, billing system. **Risk**: 🔴 thin margins + inventory
  stuck as working capital + big-brand competition; win on location + fast stock rotation.

### 10. Two-wheeler / car service franchise (ద్విచక్ర · కార్ సర్వీస్) 🚗
- **Capex ₹5–15 L**: franchise fee, tools + lift + equipment, spare stock, space deposit.
- **Economics**: **service/labour margin 30–40%**, spares 15–20%; **payback 18–24 months**;
  auto market ~8%/yr growth.
- **Franchise gives**: brand, SOPs, parts supply, training. **Risk**: 🟡 skilled mechanics +
  steady vehicle flow; win on trust, speed, AMC/service packages.

## How to read ANY franchise (the extra checklist)
1. **Franchise fee** — one-time, non-refundable usually.
2. **Royalty** — % of sales, every month, forever. Even 0% brands (Amul) exist.
3. **Deposit** — refundable? how much? (courier deposits are big but returnable).
4. **Territory** — is your area exclusive, or can they open next door?
5. **Support** — real training + supply + marketing, or just a logo?
6. **Exit / lock-in** — can you leave? any no-exit clause? (laundry warning).
7. **Their real numbers** — ask existing franchisees, don't trust the brochure.

## Common mistakes (kill list)
- Believing brochure ROI claims (aggressive, unaudited) — verify with real franchisees.
- Ignoring royalty — it eats margin every month, forever.
- Signing a no-exit / supply lock-in clause without a lawyer.
- Picking a franchise whose brand has no pull in YOUR town.
- Underfunding working capital (supermarket, QSR) — inventory + rent kill you.
- Bad location for a footfall brand (café, ice-cream, dairy).
