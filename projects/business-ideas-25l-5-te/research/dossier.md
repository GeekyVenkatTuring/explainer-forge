## The 10 rural / agri-allied ideas (Volume 5)

### 1. Small dairy farm — 10 cows (డెయిరీ ఫారం · 10 ఆవులు) 🐄
- **Capex ₹10–15 L**: 10 cows @ ₹50–80k, shed ₹1.5–3L, feed ₹20–40k/month, equipment.
- **Economics**: cow gives 10–15 L/day; net **₹3,000–5,000/cow/month** → **₹40–45k/month**;
  ROI **60–70%/yr**, payback **<2 years**.
- **Subsidy**: **NABARD DEDS 25–33%** (loan up to ₹7L @ 33%).
- **Risk**: 🔴 daily labour, animal health, feed cost, milk price; win on yield + local
  dairy/direct sales.

### 2. Layer poultry — egg farm (లేయర్ కోళ్లు · గుడ్లు) 🥚
- **Capex ₹5–15 L**: 1,000–3,000 birds, cage shed, feeders, first feed. (10,000-bird
  cage farm is bigger.)
- **Economics**: **₹6–8 profit/egg**; **₹400–500 net/bird/year**; 250–300 eggs/bird/yr;
  **payback 18–24 months**. Highest-ROI livestock model.
- **Risk**: 🔴 feed ≈ 70% cost, disease, egg-price swings; win on scale + steady mandi.

### 3. Country / Kadaknath chicken (నాటు · కడక్‌నాథ్ కోళ్లు) 🐔
- **Capex ₹3–10 L**: 500–1,000 birds, free-range/semi shed, feed (~₹300–400/bird).
- **Economics**: **₹50k–90k net/100 birds in 6 months**; native meat **₹800–1,200/kg**,
  eggs ₹20–30; low investment, high value, strong "desi" demand.
- **Risk**: 🟡 longer grow-out than broiler, marketing the premium; win on direct/hotel sales.

### 4. Piggery — pig farming (పంది పెంపకం) 🐖
- **Capex ₹1.75–10 L**: 10–40 pigs + shed + feed. (10-pig unit ₹1.75–2.5L.)
- **Economics**: **₹154 return per ₹100** invested; **~₹70k profit/10 pigs in 6–8 months**;
  pigs breed fast, grow fast, eat cheap feed/waste.
- **Risk**: 🔴 market/social acceptance, disease, feed; win on a fixed buyer + hygiene.

### 5. Sericulture — silk (పట్టు పురుగుల పెంపకం · సెరీకల్చర్) 🐛
- **Capex ₹1.5–5 L**: rearing shed (1,200 sqft ~₹1.3L), mulberry plantation, rearing
  equipment. (Excludes land.)
- **Economics**: **₹35k–45k/crop**, **4–5 crops/year/acre**; ~₹25k/month average income;
  frequent returns every ~45–50 days.
- **Subsidy**: **Central Silk Board up to 80% on rearing shed**, mulberry 50%.
- **Risk**: 🟡 silkworm care, temperature, disease; win with training + CSB support. Strong
  in AP/TS.

### 6. Ripening chamber / mini cold storage (రైపనింగ్ ఛాంబర్ · మినీ కోల్డ్ స్టోరేజ్) ❄️
- **Capex ₹10–20 L**: 100–200 MT chamber (~₹1L/MT / ₹8–15k per MT), insulation, cooling,
  power backup.
- **Economics**: **rent chambers** to farmers/traders (banana/mango ripening, veg storage);
  storage ₹1.5–3/kg/month; steady rental income.
- **Subsidy**: **NHB / MIDH 35–40%** (higher in hilly/scheduled areas).
- **Risk**: 🔴 high capex, utilisation + power; win near a mandi/production belt.

### 7. Agri-input dealership — seed/fertiliser/pesticide (అగ్రి ఇన్‌పుట్ డీలర్‌షిప్) 🌱
- **Capex ₹1–12 L**: shop + initial stock (DeHaat ₹1–3L; Gromor ₹5–12L; basic ₹35–50k).
- **Economics**: product margin + advisory commission + market-linkage; monthly income
  **₹1–3L** (Gromor); steady farmer footfall.
- **Licences**: **fertiliser/pesticide dealer licence** needs B.Sc Agri/Chem OR a 15-day
  approved training course.
- **Risk**: 🟡 credit to farmers (slow cash), seasonal; win on trust + advisory + brand tie-up.

### 8. Farm equipment rental — custom hiring centre (వ్యవసాయ పరికరాల అద్దె · CHC) 🚜
- **Capex ₹10–25 L**: tractor + implements, or a combine harvester (₹20–30L alone → keep
  the mix under 25L), tiller, rotavator, seed drill.
- **Economics**: harvester rents **₹2,000–3,000/hour**; ~500 hrs/yr → ₹10–15L; **CHC total
  ₹15–22L/year**; rent by hour/acre.
- **Subsidy**: govt **40–80% on CHC machinery** (SMAM / state).
- **Risk**: 🟡 seasonal use, maintenance; win by covering many villages + off-season crops.

### 9. Fruit / vegetable drying & dehydration (పండ్లు · కూరగాయల ఎండబెట్టడం · డీహైడ్రేషన్) 🍅
- **Capex ₹3–10 L**: dehydration machine (₹2.5L dries 150 kg/day), trays, packaging,
  (solar-dryer option cheaper).
- **Economics**: dried produce is high-value, long shelf life; **gross margin 30–40%**,
  net 12–18%; raw material ~60–70% of cost. Needs ~280–300 operating days.
- **Licences**: **FSSAI**, Udyam; **PMFME subsidy** for food processing.
- **Risk**: 🟡 raw supply + drying quality + buyer; win on dried onion/garlic/curry-leaf/
  fruit powders + B2B.

### 10. Polyhouse farming — protected cultivation (పాలీహౌస్ · గ్రీన్‌హౌస్) 🌷
- **Capex**: ₹25–40L/acre before subsidy; **after 50–75% subsidy ≈ ₹12–20L/acre** (½ acre
  fits comfortably under 25L).
- **Economics**: gerbera/rose **₹34–45L/acre/yr**, capsicum/tomato **₹12–15L/acre/yr**;
  operating cost ₹4–7L/yr; **ROI 2–3 years with subsidy**.
- **Subsidy**: **MIDH / NHB 50–75%** (some states up to 90–95%).
- **Risk**: 🔴 technical (climate control, drip, pests) + market for exotic produce; win on
  high-value flowers/veg + contract buyers.

## 90-day start plan (any idea)
1. Days 1–15 — pick by 5 lenses + your land/temperament; visit 2–3 running farms; 1-page plan.
2. Days 16–30 — Udyam + bank; identify subsidy (NABARD/NLM/CSB/NHB/MIDH/PMFME); vendor quotes.
3. Days 31–60 — arrange money (savings + loan + subsidy); build shed / buy animals-machine;
   **training + vet/agri support**.
4. Days 61–90 — first batch/crop; a fixed buyer; keep 3–6 months working capital.

## Common mistakes (kill list)
- No training on the technical ones (dairy, layer, sericulture, polyhouse) → losses.
- Ignoring the subsidy you qualify for — these ideas have unusually strong scheme support.
- All money on setup, none for feed/working capital (livestock eats daily).
- No fixed buyer / mandi linkage (chicken, pigs, drying, polyhouse produce).
- Underestimating disease/mortality — vaccinate, keep hygiene, insure livestock.
