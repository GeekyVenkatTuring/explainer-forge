# 50 to Beat the Nifty — Portfolio & Methodology

_Companion notes to the video series. **Analysis from public data — NOT investment advice.** Figures are point-in-time from screener.in (~Aug 2026) and will change. Past performance ≠ future returns. Consult a SEBI-registered advisor before investing._

## How the 50 were chosen

- **Universe:** all 2,397 NSE-listed companies (22 sectors).
- **Shortlist:** ~260 quality/growth candidates → **real fundamentals scraped** from screener.in (P/E, ROE, ROCE, market cap, 3- & 5-year sales/profit CAGR).
- **Five tests:** quality (ROE/ROCE) · growth (sales & profit CAGR) · valuation sanity (price vs growth) · moat & runway (structural tailwind) · investability (size/liquidity).
- **Three conviction tiers:** core compounders / growth accelerators / high-risk-high-reward.
- **Note:** deliberately underweights the very largest index heavyweights (owning those ≈ owning the Nifty) and omits Oil&Gas + Telecom (data unavailable at build time). This is a thesis, not a guarantee.
