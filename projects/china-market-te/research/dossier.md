# China Consumer / SME Economy — Boom Forces Dossier (Vol 1)

(macro estimates, vendor-dependent, often ranges). Educational — NOT investment advice.
Where sources conflicted, a conservative range is used and labelled "అవగాహన కోసమే".

## 0. Macro context — "why watch China"
- China 2025 GDP ≈ **RMB 140.2 trillion (~US$19.6 trillion)**; growth **5.0%** (target met, slowest in decades), decelerating 5.4% Q1 → 4.5% Q4. [China-Briefing; NBS stats.gov.cn; CNBC; Al Jazeera, Jan 2026]
- 2025 retail sales ≈ **RMB 50.12 trillion (~US$7.0 trillion)**, **+3.7%**; online retail **+8.6%**. Final consumption = **52%** of growth (up 5pp). [SCIO english.scio.gov.cn; gov.cn NBS, Jan 2026]
- China GDP ≈ **4.58×** India ($19.4T vs $4.1T, 2025); India grew **7.0%** vs China **5.0%**. [StatisticsTimes; Focus-Economics]

## Force 1 — EV & new-energy dominance
- NEV penetration ≈ **59.4%** of new passenger cars (Nov 2025); **62.9%** retail share (May 2026). [CnEVPost; CarNewsChina]
- BYD 2025 ≈ **3.48M** NEV units, **27.2%** share (down from 34.1% in 2024). Geely #2 **12.2%** (+81.3% YoY). Tesla 5th **4.9%**. [CnEVPost, Jan 2026]
- China ≈ **60%+** of all EVs sold worldwide (2024). [IEA via CnEVPost]

## Force 2 — Live-commerce scale
- Livestreaming e-commerce GMV ≈ **RMB 5 trillion (~$807bn)** in 2024, forecast **>RMB 8 trillion (>$1 trillion)** by 2026. [Statista]
- Platform share: **Douyin ~47%**, Kuaishou ~27%, Taobao Live ~23%. [ECDB; ecommercechinaagency]
- Douyin ~**700M** daily users; Kuaishou **600M+** MAU, ~100+ min/day. [Statista; getstream]

## Force 3 — Value retail / consumption shift
- **Mixue Bingcheng**: **46,000+** stores worldwide, drinks under **$1** — biggest global made-to-order drink footprint. [Xinhua; China Daily]
- "New consumption" equities (Pop Mart, **Laopu Gold**, Mixue) surged on HKEX in 2025. Laopu revenue **+167.5%** (2024) to >8.5bn yuan, **+251%** H1 2025. [China.org; China Daily]
- Pinduoduo = value-retail anchor; consumers "rationalising", not simply downgrading (McKinsey mid-2025).

## Force 4 — New consumption / experience economy
- **64%** of consumers prioritise emotional fulfilment; Gen Z drives self-care, collectibles, experiences. [Accio; Octoplus]
- **Pet economy**: ¥295bn (2020) → est. **¥1.15 trillion (2028)**. [China Daily]
- Guzi (collectibles) economy, Pop Mart; travel ≈ **60%** of purchases online. [China Daily; McKinsey]

## Force 5 — Silver economy & aging
- 60+ population **310 million = 22%** of total (end-2024), **+12M/yr**. [Xinhua; gov.cn]
- Silver economy 2025 est. **~RMB 7–9 trillion** now (MIIT white-paper cites up to 15.8T on a broad basis); could reach **~RMB 30 trillion by 2035**; growing **>10%/yr**. [Time; Global Times; Made-in-China; Fudan Institute on Aging]

## Force 6 — Guochao (domestic-brand pride)
- Guochao market est. **>RMB 3 trillion by 2028**; strongest among **270M+** Gen Z / millennials. [Daxue; Statista]
- Winners: **Li-Ning, Anta** (sportswear), beauty, gaming. Guochao 3.0 = chosen for **quality/innovation**, not only patriotism. [hubofchina; Daxue]

## Force 7 — Clean-tech export machine ("New Three")
- Clean-tech exports record **$20bn in Aug 2025**; ≈**$120bn** green-tech through Jul 2025 (> US oil & gas exports). [Ember; Electrek; SCMP]
- "New Three" = EV + battery + solar. EV exports **+26%**, battery **+23/24%** (Jan–Sep, ~$60bn), solar PV value **−19%** but **46 GW in Aug alone** (record volume). [Ember; Electrek; Glottis]
- **CATL >35%** global EV-battery share. Clean energy drove **>1/3** of China's 2025 GDP growth. [Carbon Brief; CATL]

## Force 8 — AI & advanced manufacturing
- **DeepSeek** (low-cost models) + **UBTech** humanoids (DeepSeek-R1 based) deployed in factories. [Techi; Carnegie]
- China allocated **>$20bn** to humanoids incl. a **1 trillion yuan ($137bn)** fund; 2025 Action Plan targets **100,000** deployed by 2027. [Carnegie; IFR; Merics]
- 2025: China produced **12,800 humanoids (~90%** of global). 15+ automakers entered the race. [Carnegie]

## Force 9 — Cross-border e-commerce
- Cross-border e-commerce trade **RMB 2.75 trillion (2025)**, **+70%** vs 2020; exports ≈**RMB 2 trillion** (>70% of total). [SCMP; Cross-Border Magazine]
- **Shein ~9%**, AliExpress ~8%, **Temu** catching Amazon in global cross-border. [SCMP; Caixin]
- Headwind: US closed the **$800 de-minimis** (2025); EU proposing **€2/parcel** fee. [Cross-Border Magazine]

## Force 10 — Property slowdown & consumer ripple (the shadow)
- Real-estate investment **−13.9% YoY** (first 3 quarters 2025). Housing = **~70%** of urban household assets. [Modern Diplomacy; PIIE]
- A 10% home-price fall cuts household spending ~**1.5–2.3%**; property drag ≈ **−2ppt/yr** off GDP (2024–25). [Atlantic Council; Stanford SCCEI]
