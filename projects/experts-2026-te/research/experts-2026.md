## Samir Arora — Helios Capital (@Iamsamirarora)
Source: ValueResearch, BusinessToday.
- "Elimination strategy": easier to know who the losers are than the winners → systematically avoid

- "India's normal is not bad" — historical earnings growth 13–15% in rupee terms; projects ~10–12%
  aggregate earnings growth.
- Portfolio tilt: consumer-habit-change firms (e.g., CarTrade, PB Fintech, Delhivery).

## Saurabh Mukherjea — Marcellus (@ssundresh)
Source: Whalesbook, Marcellus newsletter, PMS AIF World.

- Marcellus AUM ~₹2,798 cr (Feb 2026).

## Nilesh Shah — Kotak Mahindra AMC MD (@NileshShah68)
Source: Zee Business.

- Sample multi-asset: ~60% equity / 20% gold & silver / 20% debt.
- Market corrections = opportunity for long-term investors; large-cap funds ideal for SIP, multi-asset

## Deepak Shenoy — Capitalmind (@deepakshenoy)
Source: Capitalmind, ALTPORT profile.

## Common thread (synthesis, honest)
1. Quality over hype — trim overheated small/mid-caps, tilt to quality large-caps.
2. Asset allocation > stock picking (Nilesh Shah's 90/10; Shenoy's debt/equity switching).
3. Discipline over emotion — rules-based (Shenoy), risk control/elimination (Arora).
4. Stay invested in India's steady ~10–15% earnings-growth story; no crash expected; corrections = opportunity.
