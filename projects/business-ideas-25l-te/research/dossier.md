# Business Ideas Under ₹25 Lakh — Dossier (verified 2026-08-01)

All figures are **indicative industry ranges**, cross-checked across multiple 2025–26
trade/vendor/project-report sources. Real cost varies by **city, scale, vendor,
automation, and whether land/space is owned or rented**. Presented on screen as
RANGES, never as promises. **Educational only — NOT financial / investment advice.**
Margins are typical; a first-year operator often earns less until utilisation ramps.

Audience: aspiring first-time founder, comfortable in Andhra Pradesh / Telangana,
budget **under ₹25 lakh**. Language on screen & narration: Telugu + English (Tanglish);
brand names, ₹ amounts, %, numbers stay Latin.

## Part 1 — The lens (framework)

### The 5 questions to judge ANY business (semantic identity colors)
1. **పెట్టుబడి (Investment / capex)** — one-time money to set up (machinery, deposit,
   licences) + how much is *working capital* (stock, salaries, credit given).
2. **గ్రాస్ మార్జిన్ (Gross margin)** — of every ₹100 of sales, how much is left after
   the direct cost of the product. Thin-margin businesses (detergent, water) win on
   VOLUME; fat-margin ones (mushroom, printing) win on smaller sales.
3. **పేబ్యాక్ / ROI (Payback)** — months to earn back the capex. Under-25L ideas here
   range ~3 months (mushroom) to ~24 months (water plant).
4. **వర్కింగ్ క్యాపిటల్ (Working capital / cash cycle)** — money stuck in stock +
   credit given to buyers *before* cash comes back. This, not machinery, kills most
   small units. Food/agri = fast cash; B2B supply = slow cash.
5. **మోట్ & రిస్క్ (Moat & risk)** — what protects you (licence, location, brand,
   recipe) and what can sink you (platform commission, disease, price of one raw
   material, one big competitor).
- Extra lens: **temperament** — do you like machines, food, farming, selling, kids,
  screens? Pick the one you will still run on a bad day.

### Money & licences — common ground (all ideas)
- **Udyam registration** — free, online, ~10 min. The MSME identity; **gateway** to
  MUDRA/PMEGP, subsidies, GST benefits. Do this first.
- **GST** — mandatory once turnover crosses threshold or for interstate / marketplace
  selling (Amazon/Flipkart require it from day one).
- **MUDRA loan (PMMY)** — collateral-free up to **₹10 lakh** for micro business.
  Three steps: Shishu (≤₹50k), Kishore (₹50k–₹5L), Tarun (₹5L–₹10L). It's a *loan* —
  **no subsidy**, you repay full + interest.
- **PMEGP** — govt scheme with **margin-money subsidy 15–35%** (higher for
  SC/ST/women/rural), project up to **₹50L (manufacturing) / ₹20L (service)**. Udyam
  needed. Only one person per family. Subsidy released after ~3 yrs lock-in.
- **FSSAI** — mandatory for ANY food business (cloud kitchen, namkeen, water, mushroom
  if packed).
- **BIS / ISI mark** — mandatory for packaged drinking water; needed for some products.
- **Pollution CTE/CTO (TSPCB / APPCB)** — for manufacturing (water, cups, detergent,
  poultry above a size).
- **Shops & Establishment + Trade licence + Fire NOC** — local municipal, most units.

## Part 2 — The 10 ideas (indicative)

Legend for on-screen risk/effort chips: 🟢 easy/low · 🟡 medium · 🔴 hard/high.

### 1. Cloud kitchen (క్లౌడ్ కిచెన్) — food service
- **Capex ₹5–12 L**: kitchen equipment ₹2–4L, rent deposit (200–400 sqft, no dine-in),
  packaging, branding, licences, ~2 months working capital.
- **Unit economics** (₹250 order): food cost ~₹80 + packaging ~₹15 + **platform
  commission 25–30% (~₹65)** + ads; contribution ≈ ₹35–45/order. **Net margin ~10–15%**
  at volume. **Break-even ~6–8 months.**
- **Licences**: FSSAI, GST, Shops&Estab, trade, (fire if needed).
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 heavy dependence on Zomato/Swiggy commission + discounts; win with
  a tight menu, repeat customers, own WhatsApp/direct orders, low rent.

### 2. Packaged / RO drinking water plant (ప్యాకేజ్డ్ వాటర్ ప్లాంట్) — manufacturing
- **Capex ₹12–25 L**: 1000–2000 LPH RO + treatment machinery ₹8–15L, jars/bottles/
  caps/labels, shed/plumbing, BIS+FSSAI, working capital.
- **Margins**: gross **25–40%**; **ROI 18–24 months.** Distribution (jars to homes/
  offices) is the real business.
- **Licences**: **BIS/ISI (mandatory for packaged water)**, FSSAI, pollution CTE/CTO,
  GST, Udyam.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 BIS compliance strict, local price wars, delivery logistics + jar
  returns; steady repeat demand once a route is built.

### 3. Paper cup / paper plate manufacturing (పేపర్ కప్ తయారీ) — manufacturing
- **Capex ₹8–18 L**: machine 40–120 cups/min ₹4–9L (refurbished route keeps all-in
  **under ₹12L** if space owned), PE-coated paper reels, dies, working capital.
- **Unit economics**: make ~**₹0.20/cup**, sell ~**₹0.30/cup** → ~₹0.10 GP; **gross
  30–40%, net 12–18%.** A single machine ≈ 50,000+ cups/day.
- **Tailwind**: single-use *plastic* ban → paper demand rising.
- **Licences**: Udyam, GST, factory/trade, pollution consent.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 paper-reel price swings, power, crowded market; win on consistent
  quality + B2B tie-ups (tea stalls, caterers, brands).

### 4. Namkeen / snacks / masala unit (నమ్‌కీన్ · మసాలా యూనిట్) — food processing
- **Capex ₹3–8 L** small (fryer, mixer, sealing/packaging); a spice grinding+blending
  unit runs higher (₹5–15L).
- **Margins 18–30%; ROI ~8–12 months.**
- **Licences**: **FSSAI (mandatory)**, GST, Udyam.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 shelf life, quality consistency, strong local brands; win on taste,
  hygiene, local distribution, and a hero product.

### 5. Detergent & cleaning products (డిటర్జెంట్ · ఫినాయిల్ · లిక్విడ్) — light mfg
- **Capex ₹2–8 L** (mixing tanks, filling; can start home-scale ₹50k–1L).
- **Margins**: powder thin **6–15%**, liquid **12–20%** — a **high-volume, low-margin**
  game; money is in selling *quantity* consistently.
- **Licences**: Udyam, GST, trade, (BIS optional), minimal pollution.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 giant brands (Surf, Ghadi, Rin) + thin margins; win ONLY on local
  distribution, refill/institutional supply, and cost control.

### 6. Broiler poultry farming (బ్రాయిలర్ కోళ్ల ఫారం) — agri (AP/TS strong)
- **Capex ₹6–15 L** for 1000–2000 birds: shed 2000–3000 sqft (₹3–10L), feeders/
  drinkers/brooders, first batch chicks (~₹48/chick) + feed.
- **Economics**: **₹25–40 profit/bird on contract** (integrator like Suguna/Venky's),
  **₹50–90/bird independent**; **6–7 batches/year.** **Feed ≈ 70% of running cost.**
- **Licences**: pollution NOC, panchayat/local, (contract with integrator common).
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 disease/mortality, price volatility, feed cost; contract farming
  lowers price risk but caps upside.

### 7. Mushroom farming (పుట్టగొడుగుల సాగు) — agri, lowest entry
- **Capex ₹40k–5 L**: single room (oyster, no cooling) to a small commercial unit
  (humidifier, racks, spawn, bags).
- **Economics**: sell **₹120–250/kg**; margins high; **payback ~3–6 months.** Oyster
  ready ~45 days, 6–8 cycles/year.
- **Licences**: minimal (Udyam; FSSAI if packed/processed).
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 contamination, and *perishability/marketing* is the hard part; needs
  training + a fixed buyer (hotels, retail, drying for value-add).

### 8. Digital / flex printing shop (డిజిటల్ · ఫ్లెక్స్ ప్రింటింగ్) — services
- **Capex ₹2–8 L**: flex/eco-solvent printer, computer + design, lamination, shop
  deposit.
- **Margins 20–35%.**
- **Licences**: Udyam, GST, shops/trade.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 local competition, machine maintenance, uneven order flow; win with
  design skill, speed, and B2B/event tie-ups.

### 9. Play school / preschool (ప్లే స్కూల్) — services
- **Capex ₹8–20 L**: setup + toys/furniture + deposit; optional **franchise fee ₹3–5L**
  (brand + curriculum + training).
- **Economics**: fee **₹2,000–4,000/child/month**; **operating margin 20–35%**;
  **payback ~12–18 months.**
- **Licences**: local registration, trade, fire, safety; no CBSE affiliation needed for
  pre-primary.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 location + safety + reputation are everything; seasonal admissions;
  a franchise cuts risk but shares revenue.

### 10. D2C / private-label e-commerce (D2C · ప్రైవేట్ లేబెల్) — online retail
- **Capex ₹2–10 L**: inventory (supplier MOQ), branding/packaging, listing, initial ads.
  Scrappy start possible near ₹1–1.5L.
- **Economics**: marketplace commission **15–30%** (Amazon/Flipkart/Meesho); category
  decides margin; **ad cost (CAC) + returns** are the silent killers.
- **Licences**: **GST (mandatory)**, Udyam, trademark for the brand, FSSAI/BIS if the
  category needs it.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 rising platform + ad fees, working capital stuck in stock; win with
  a differentiated product, good reviews, and moving buyers to your own site/repeat.

### 90-day start plan (any idea)
1. **Days 1–15** — decide idea by the 5 lenses + your temperament; validate demand
   (talk to 20 buyers), draft a 1-page numbers plan.
2. **Days 16–30** — Udyam + bank account + GST; choose site/space; get quotes.
3. **Days 31–60** — arrange money (savings + MUDRA/PMEGP); order machine; apply FSSAI/
   BIS/pollution as needed.
4. **Days 61–90** — install + trial production; first customers; keep 3–6 months of
   working capital as a cushion. Start small, prove unit economics, THEN scale.

### Common mistakes (kill list)
- Spending all money on machinery, none on **working capital**.
- Chasing the biggest capex idea instead of the one that fits your **skill + cash**.
- Ignoring **licences** (FSSAI/BIS/pollution) → raids, fines, shutdown.
- Underpricing to win early orders → never reaching break-even margin.
- No fixed buyer / distribution plan (esp. mushroom, cups, detergent).
