## Apps / strengths
- **Zerodha** — Kite (pro trading), Coin (2,000+ direct MF, consolidated XIRR across MF+equity), Console,
  Varsity (free education), Sensibull (options). Best ecosystem / all-rounder.
- **Groww** — simplest app, #1 by users; MF needs only KYC (no demat); free direct MF (first ₹25k free).
  Best for beginners & MF. Power-users find it limited; it charges on delivery.
- **Dhan** — new-age (2021); #1 options app 2026: native TradingView charts, options chain, strategy tools,
  fast. ₹0 delivery/MF, ₹0 AMC. Best for F&O/options traders.
- **Angel One** — strong research reports & smart suggestions; ₹240 AMC. Complaint: heavy notifications.
- **Fyers** — chart-heavy, API/algo trading.

## Recent user-reported issues (Reddit/Twitter/news)
- **Cloudflare outage — 5 Dec 2025**: Zerodha, Angel One, Upstox, Groww all disrupted ~12 min — login,
  order-placement, data failures. THIRD-PARTY (Cloudflare) cause, not the brokers'. Zerodha pointed users
  to Kite's WhatsApp backup; Groww posted status updates.
- **Zerodha** — historic Kite slowdowns on high-volume/expiry days (#KiteDown); largely improved since.
- **Groww** — charges ₹20 on delivery (peers free); occasional charge/statement disputes.
- **Angel One** — intraday auto square-off at 3:15 PM; complaints of aggressive notifications / mis-selling.
- Frame all as user-reported; brokers have largely addressed them; funds/shares remain safe (SEBI/NSDL/CDSL).

## Best per use case
- **Mutual funds**: Groww (simple, ₹0, no demat) for beginners; Zerodha Coin (XIRR, ecosystem) if you also trade; Kuvera MF-only.
- **Stocks / delivery**: Zerodha or Dhan (₹0 delivery + ₹0/low AMC + good tools). Avoid Groww for large delivery volumes (it charges).
- **Futures & Options**: Dhan (#1 options app, TradingView, speed) or Zerodha (Kite + Sensibull, reliability); Upstox/Fyers for active/algo.
- **All-in-one**: Zerodha (complete ecosystem); Dhan as the modern challenger.
