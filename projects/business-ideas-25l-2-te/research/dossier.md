# Business Ideas Under ₹25 Lakh — VOLUME 2 (the less-crowded ideas) — Dossier (verified 2026-08-01)

Sequel to Video 1. Video 1 covered the popular, well-trodden ideas (cloud kitchen,
water plant, paper cup, namkeen, detergent, poultry, mushroom, flex printing, play
school, D2C). Feedback: "good, but everybody's already doing them." So **Volume 2 =
10 ideas that are still emerging / less saturated in AP-TS**, each riding a real
2025–26 tailwind (drones, EV, millets, organic, aqua-tech, D2C-content).

All figures are **indicative industry ranges**, cross-checked across 2025–26
trade/vendor/project-report sources. Real cost varies by **city, scale, vendor,
automation, and whether land/space is owned or rented**. Shown on screen as RANGES,
never promises. **Educational only — NOT financial / investment advice.** A first-year
operator usually earns less until utilisation ramps.

Audience: aspiring first-time founder in Andhra Pradesh / Telangana, budget **under
₹25 lakh**. Screen + narration: Telugu + English (Tanglish); brands, ₹, %, numbers
stay Latin.

Reused framework (from Video 1): judge ANY business by **5 lenses** — పెట్టుబడి (capex),
గ్రాస్ మార్జిన్, పేబ్యాక్/ROI, వర్కింగ్ క్యాపిటల్ (cash cycle), మోట్ & రిస్క్ — plus your
temperament. Common setup for all: **Udyam** (free), **GST**, **MUDRA** (collateral-free
≤₹10L), **PMEGP** (15–35% margin-money subsidy), **FSSAI** (any food), sector licences.

Legend for risk/effort chips: 🟢 easy/low · 🟡 medium · 🔴 hard/high.

## The 10 ideas (Volume 2)

### 1. Agri-drone spraying service (డ్రోన్ స్ప్రేయింగ్ సర్వీస్) — agri-tech service 🚁
- **Capex ₹5–15 L**: agri spray drone (10–20 L tank) ₹4–8L, spare battery + fast
  charger ₹1–2L, generator, DGCA remote-pilot training ~₹50k, transport, working capital.
- **Subsidy is the story**: SMAM **40–50%** (FPO/co-op/SC-ST higher), **Namo Drone Didi
  up to 80% / ₹8L** for women SHGs. A ₹10L drone can cost an FPO ~₹2.5L.
- **Economics**: charge **₹400–700/acre**; spray 10–15 acre/day; ~200–300 acre/month in
  season → revenue **₹1–1.8 L/month**; running cost ₹20–30k → net **₹60k–1.5L/month at
  peak**. Highly **seasonal** (kharif/rabi spray windows).
- **Licences**: **DGCA remote-pilot certificate + drone (DAN) registration**, agri-input
  tie-ups; Kisan Drone / SMAM enrolment.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 seasonal demand, battery life, pilot skill + farmer trust; being the
  **first drone in a mandal + subsidy** is the moat.

### 2. Cold-pressed / wood-ghani oil unit (కోల్డ్-ప్రెస్డ్ · చెక్క గానుగ నూనె) — food processing 🛢️
- **Capex ₹3–8 L**: cold-press/ghani machine ₹2–6L, filter press, bottling/filling,
  FSSAI, branding, working capital (seeds — groundnut/sesame/coconut).
- **Economics**: cost ~**₹100–150/L**, sells **₹200–450/L** (premium, "chekka ganuga").
  Gross margin **30–50%**; **payback ~3–6 months** at steady sales.
- **Licences**: **FSSAI (mandatory)**, Udyam, GST.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 built on **purity/trust brand + direct/D2C** selling; risk = seed
  price, low awareness, marketing. Rides the health/clean-label tailwind.

### 3. Sanitary-napkin manufacturing (శానిటరీ న్యాప్‌కిన్ తయారీ) — light mfg 🩹
- **Capex ₹2–20 L**: semi-auto ₹2–8L (80–100 pads/min, manual steps), fully-auto
  ₹8–20L; raw material (wood pulp, SAP gel, non-woven top sheet), moulds, working capital.
- **Economics**: raw material ~**50–60%** of cost; gross **20–40%**, net **~15–25%** at
  scale; **payback 12–18 months**.
- **Licences**: GST, Udyam, **BIS / lab quality test**, trademark. (Not a food unit — no
  FSSAI.)
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 giant brands (Whisper/Stayfree) + ₹1 govt Suvidha pads; win on
  **institutional/local supply** (schools, ASHA/SHG, govt tenders) and low-cost niche.
  Strong social angle.

### 4. Biofloc fish farming (బయోఫ్లాక్ చేపల పెంపకం) — aqua-tech (AP strong) 🐟
- **Capex ₹3–10 L**: 6–10 tanks (10–20k L), aerators/blowers, borewell, shed,
  fingerlings, feed, **power backup**.
- **Economics**: tilapia/rohu/pangasius; **~6-month cycle**, ~**400–600 kg/tank**, sell
  **₹150–300/kg**; net **₹80k–1.5L/cycle** for a small unit. **Payback ~12–18 months.**
- **Licences**: **PMMSY subsidy 40–60%**, local fisheries dept, water/pollution.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 **technical** — water chemistry, dissolved oxygen; a **power cut =
  mass mortality**; steep learning. De-risk with training + backup power + a fixed buyer.

### 5. Millet-based products unit (మిల్లెట్ ఉత్పత్తుల యూనిట్) — food processing 🌾
- **Capex ₹5–15 L**: cleaning/grading, flour mill, ready-mix blender, roaster, sealing/
  packaging — a **small value-add unit** (NOT a ₹40–55L full processing plant).
- **Economics**: raw millet is low margin; **branded processed** (millet flour, ready-mix
  idli/dosa, health mix, snacks, laddu) → **25–40%**. **Payback ~10–15 months.**
- **Licences**: **FSSAI (mandatory)**, Udyam, GST.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 branding + distribution + shelf life are the hard part; rides the
  **millet health + govt push** tailwind (Intl Year of Millets legacy).

### 6. Vermicompost / organic-manure unit (వర్మీకంపోస్ట్ · సేంద్రియ ఎరువు) — agri, low capex ♻️
- **Capex ₹1–8 L**: beds/shed, worms (₹250/kg), cow-dung supply, shredder, packaging;
  a rural unit does **10–30 t/month**.
- **Economics**: cost **< ₹2/kg**; sells bulk **₹4–8/kg**, branded/retail **₹12–20/kg**;
  margin **20–45%**; **payback ~10–15 months**. **NABARD/state subsidy 50–75%.**
- **Licences**: Udyam, **Fertiliser Control Order (FCO)** registration to sell as
  fertiliser, GST.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 bulk sales are thin — money is in a **branded channel + steady dung
  supply**; rides the organic-farming tailwind.

### 7. Beekeeping & honey (తేనెటీగల పెంపకం · తేనె) — agri, very low capex 🐝
- **Capex ₹2–5 L**: 50–100 boxes @ ~₹3,500 each + bee colonies, protective gear,
  honey extractor, storage, bottling. **NBHM/KVIB/state subsidy up to 80–85%.**
- **Economics**: ~**30–40 kg honey/box/year**; honey **₹250–500/kg**; a 50-box unit nets
  **₹1.5–4 L/year**. Value-add: beeswax, bee pollen, propolis, royal jelly.
- **Licences**: Udyam, **FSSAI** (packed honey), **NBB** registration.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 seasonal + migratory management, disease, needs training; marketing
  **branded vs raw** honey decides the margin.

### 8. Corrugated-box unit (కొరుగేటెడ్ బాక్స్ తయారీ) — B2B mfg, e-commerce tailwind 📦
- **Capex ₹10–25 L**: **semi-auto conversion unit** (sheet cutting, creasing, printing,
  stitching/pasting) buying corrugated sheets/2-ply reels; a full auto reel-to-box line
  costs much more.
- **Economics**: **kraft/board = 70–80% of cost**; gross **19–26%**, net **12–18%**;
  **payback ~18–24 months.**
- **Licences**: Udyam, GST, factory/trade, pollution consent.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 kraft price swings, **B2B credit = slow cash**; needs steady buyers
  (e-commerce, pharma, FMCG) — best sited near an industrial cluster.

### 9. EV charging station (EV ఛార్జింగ్ స్టేషన్) — green / services tailwind ⚡
- **Capex**: AC slow ₹1.5–5L; **DC fast 30–60 kW ₹10–25L**; franchise fee ₹1–2.5L
  (Tata Power / Jio-bp) OR independent; + civil work, DISCOM power connection, land/lease.
- **Economics**: **₹15–25/kWh**; 100–200 kWh/day (urban); net **₹60k–1.2L/month** for a
  fast charger; margin **15–30%**; **payback 2.5–5 years** (faster Tier-1, slower Tier-2).
- **Licences**: selling EV charging is **de-licensed** (no electricity licence), but needs
  **DISCOM connection, land, fire/safety, MoP guidelines**.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 **location + utilisation is everything**, long payback, tech changes;
  co-locate with retail/food. India needs ~46,000 public chargers by 2030 (<15k in 2025).

### 10. Digital-marketing / content micro-agency (డిజిటల్ మార్కెటింగ్ · కంటెంట్ ఏజెన్సీ) — pure services 💻
- **Capex ₹0.5–3 L**: laptop, software subscriptions, camera/mic, initial ads, small
  workspace. **Lowest capex on this list.**
- **Economics**: retainers **₹15k–2L/month/client**; per-reel ₹2–8k; margin **20–40%**;
  near-zero payback (**skill-funded**, not machine-funded).
- **Licences**: Udyam, GST (once threshold crossed); no special licence.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 **skill + client churn + sales** — the machine is you; win on a
  **niche** (local clinics, real estate, restaurants), a portfolio, and results.

## 90-day start plan (any idea)
1. **Days 1–15** — pick by the 5 lenses + temperament; validate demand (talk to 20
   buyers/farmers/clients); draft a 1-page numbers plan.
2. **Days 16–30** — Udyam + bank account + GST; choose site/space; get vendor quotes;
   check the matching subsidy (SMAM/Drone Didi/PMMSY/NBHM/NABARD/PMEGP).
3. **Days 31–60** — arrange money (savings + MUDRA/PMEGP + scheme subsidy); order
   machine/drone/tanks; apply FSSAI/BIS/FCO/DGCA/DISCOM as needed; get **training**.
4. **Days 61–90** — install + trial run; first customers; keep **3–6 months working
   capital** cushion. Start small, prove unit economics, THEN scale.

## Common mistakes (kill list)
- Chasing the biggest capex idea instead of the one that fits your **skill + cash**.
- Skipping **training** on the technical ones (drone, biofloc, beekeeping) → costly failure.
- All money on the machine, none on **working capital** / marketing.
- Ignoring the **subsidy** you qualify for (these 10 have unusually strong scheme support).
- No fixed buyer / distribution plan (vermicompost, corrugated box, millet, honey).
- For services (drone, EV, agency): under-utilisation — a machine that sits idle still
  costs money.
