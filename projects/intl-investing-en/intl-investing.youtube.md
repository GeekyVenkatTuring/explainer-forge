## Description
Yes — you can legally invest in US and international stocks from India. This is the complete,
end-to-end guide: whether it's possible, the exact platforms, every single cost of buying and
selling, and how tax works on your dividends and gains.

We cover:
• Is it legal? — the RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), up to $250,000 per person per year
• How your rupees actually reach Wall Street (the LRS money-pipe, step by step)
• The platforms — Indian apps (INDmoney, Vested, Groww) vs global/full-service (Interactive Brokers, ICICI Direct Global, GIFT City), compared
• Every cost — forex markup, TCS, brokerage, and the repatriation cost people forget
• TCS decoded — 20% only above ₹10 lakh/year, and it's fully refundable
• Taxes on returns — 25% US dividend withholding (with Foreign Tax Credit), and capital gains taxed in India (LTCG 12.5% > 24 months, STCG at slab)
• Compliance — Schedule FA is mandatory
• The indirect route — India-listed ETFs and mutual funds, in rupees, no LRS
• The real risks nobody advertises — currency, costs on small sums, access pauses, compliance

Key numbers (verified for FY 2025-26; rules/fees can change — always check current sources):
– LRS limit: $250,000 per person per financial year
– TCS on investment: 20% above ₹10 lakh/year, refundable/adjustable in your ITR
– US dividends: 25% withheld, credited under the India-US DTAA
– Capital gains (resident): India only — LTCG 12.5% (>24 months, no indexation), STCG at slab
– Schedule FA disclosure of foreign holdings: mandatory

⚠️ Education only — NOT investment advice. No stock, platform or fund here is a recommendation.
Consult a SEBI-registered advisor / chartered accountant for your own situation.
