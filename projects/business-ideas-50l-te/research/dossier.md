# Business Ideas Under ₹50 Lakh — Volume 1 — Dossier (compiled 2026-08-06)

The **bigger tier**. The under-₹25-lakh series covered small/home/low-capex starts. This
new ₹50-lakh series is the **step up**: mid-size businesses with real machinery, a small
team, a proper location, and stronger moats — capex mostly in the **₹25–50 lakh** band.

All figures are **indicative industry ranges** (2025–26), presented on screen as RANGES,
never as promises. Real cost varies hugely by **city, scale, vendor, automation, and
whether land/space is owned or rented**. Margins are typical at healthy utilisation; a
first-year operator usually earns less until the unit ramps. **Educational only — NOT
financial / investment advice.** Verify with local vendors, a CA, and current scheme rules
before investing.

Audience: an aspiring owner in Andhra Pradesh / Telangana with **₹25–50 lakh** to deploy
(own savings + a bank/MUDRA-plus/term loan). Language: Telugu + English (Tanglish); brand
names, ₹ amounts, %, numbers stay Latin on screen.

## Part 1 — The lens (what changes at the ₹50 lakh tier)

At ₹25–50 lakh the game is different from a small start. The 5 questions still apply, but
the emphasis shifts:

1. **పెట్టుబడి mix (capex vs working capital)** — bigger machinery bills, but the killer is
   still working capital: stock + salaries + credit given to buyers. Keep **6–9 months** of
   runway. Never spend the whole ₹50L on machines.
2. **యుటిలైజేషన్ (Utilisation / capacity)** — a ₹40L machine or a 60-seat restaurant only
   makes money when it runs full. The whole tier lives or dies on **capacity utilisation**;
   model break-even at 40–50% utilisation, not 100%.
3. **పేబ్యాక్ / ROI** — bigger tier = **longer payback**: typically **18–36 months** (vs
   3–12 months for the small tier). Plan for it.
4. **టీమ్ & సిస్టమ్స్ (Team & systems)** — you now hire 4–15 people. Hiring, training,
   attrition, and SOPs become the real job. The owner becomes a manager.
5. **మోట్ & రిస్క్** — the moat is bigger here (machinery + location + brand + B2B
   contracts + licences), but so is the downside — a wrong location or idle capacity burns
   cash fast.
- **Temperament**: this tier needs someone who can run a team and hold nerve through a
  slow ramp — not a solo hustler.

### Money & licences — common ground
- **Udyam registration** (free, ~10 min) — the MSME identity; gateway to schemes.
- **GST** — mandatory at this scale (turnover + B2B + interstate).
- **Bank term loan / CGTMSE** — collateral-free cover up to **₹5 crore** under CGTMSE for
  MSME term loans; most ₹25–50L projects are bank-funded (own margin 20–30% + loan).
- **PMEGP** — margin-money subsidy **15–35%**, project up to **₹50L (manufacturing) /
  ₹20L (service)** — directly relevant to this tier's manufacturing ideas.
- **FSSAI** (any food), **Factory/Pollution CTE-CTO** (manufacturing), **Shops &
  Establishment + Trade licence + Fire NOC** (most units), plus idea-specific licences.

## Part 2 — The 10 ideas (indicative, ₹25–50 L)

Legend for on-screen risk/effort chips: 🟢 easy/low · 🟡 medium · 🔴 hard/high.

### 1. Full-service family restaurant (ఫుల్-సర్వీస్ రెస్టారెంట్) — dine-in, 50–80 covers 🍽️
- **Capex ₹25–45 L**: kitchen equipment ₹6–12L, interiors/seating/AC ₹8–18L, rent deposit
  (1500–2500 sqft) ₹3–6L, branding/POS/licences, ~2–3 months working capital.
- **Unit economics**: gross (food) margin **60–70%**; after rent + salaries + power + gas,
  **net 10–18%** at healthy footfall. A stable outlet does **₹8–20L revenue/month**.
- **Payback ~18–30 months.**
- **Licences**: FSSAI, GST, Shops & Estab, trade, fire NOC, (bar licence if serving liquor).
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 location + food consistency + service; rent and idle tables kill it.
  Win on a tight menu, repeat diners, delivery add-on, and a strong dinner/weekend crowd.

### 2. Plastic / PVC products manufacturing (ప్లాస్టిక్ · PVC తయారీ) — injection moulding 🏭
- **Capex ₹30–50 L**: injection-moulding machine(s) ₹15–30L, moulds/dies ₹4–10L, raw
  material (granules), shed/power connection, working capital.
- **Unit economics**: convert granules to products (household ware, caps, fittings, crates);
  **gross 25–35%, net 12–20%**; raw material ≈ 60–70% of cost. Runs on **volume + B2B orders**.
- **Payback ~24–36 months.**
- **Licences**: Udyam, GST, factory/trade, pollution CTE-CTO, BIS (some products).
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 granule price swings, power cost, machine uptime; win on steady B2B
  buyers, a niche product, and consistent quality. PMEGP subsidy fits well here.

### 3. Modular furniture & interiors workshop (మాడ్యులర్ ఫర్నిచర్ · ఇంటీరియర్స్) — B2C+B2B 🪑
- **Capex ₹25–45 L**: CNC/panel saw + edge-bander + basic machines ₹10–20L, workshop
  space + tools, showroom/display ₹5–10L, raw boards (ply/MDF/laminate), working capital.
- **Unit economics**: modular kitchens/wardrobes priced per sqft; **gross 30–45%, net
  15–25%**; a kitchen project ₹1.5–4L each. Design + finish quality drive premium.
- **Payback ~18–30 months.**
- **Licences**: Udyam, GST, trade, pollution (dust), fire.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 skilled carpenters + design + on-time delivery; win on builder/architect
  tie-ups, a good showroom, and referrals. Rides the housing + interiors boom.

### 4. Frozen food / ready-to-eat (RTE) processing unit (ఫ్రోజెన్ ఫుడ్ · RTE) — food processing 🧊
- **Capex ₹30–50 L**: blast freezer + cold room ₹12–22L, processing/packing line, deep
  freezers for distribution, FSSAI-grade setup, working capital.
- **Unit economics**: frozen parathas, momos, snacks, RTE curries; **gross 30–45%, net
  15–22%**; long shelf life + branded retail/HORECA supply. Cold-chain logistics is the job.
- **Payback ~24–36 months.**
- **Licences**: **FSSAI (mandatory)**, GST, Udyam, pollution, cold-storage norms.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 cold-chain + distribution + shelf presence; win on a hero SKU, HORECA +
  modern-trade tie-ups, and consistent quality. Rides the frozen/convenience-food tailwind.

### 5. Multi-brand car service & detailing centre (కార్ సర్వీస్ · డిటైలింగ్ సెంటర్) — auto service 🚗
- **Capex ₹30–50 L**: 2–3 lifts + wheel aligner + balancer + tools ₹12–22L, washing bay +
  detailing setup, spare-parts stock, space deposit (3000–5000 sqft), working capital.
- **Unit economics**: labour margin **35–45%**, spares 15–20%, detailing/ceramic high
  margin; a busy centre services **8–15 cars/day**, revenue **₹6–15L/month**.
- **Payback ~24–36 months.**
- **Licences**: Udyam, GST, trade, pollution NOC (used oil), fire.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 skilled mechanics + steady vehicle flow + trust; win on AMC/service
  packages, insurance-claim tie-ups, and detailing upsell. India's car parc keeps growing.

### 6. Diagnostic lab + pharmacy (డయాగ్నోస్టిక్ ల్యాబ్ · ఫార్మసీ) — neighbourhood healthcare 💊
- **Capex ₹25–45 L**: semi-auto analyzers (biochem, haematology, etc.) ₹12–22L, or a
  collection-centre + pharmacy model (lighter); interiors, medicine stock ₹5–8L, software.
- **Unit economics**: lab tests **gross 45–60%**; pharmacy 15–25%; a combined point near
  clinics/hospitals does **₹4–10L revenue/month**, net **20–30%**.
- **Payback ~24–36 months.**
- **Licences**: **Drug licence (pharmacy)**, **clinical-establishment / pathology
  registration**, qualified pharmacist + lab technician, GST, Udyam, biomedical-waste tie-up.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 licences + a qualified team + doctor referrals; win on location near
  hospitals, home-collection, and tie-ups. Health-diagnostics demand is structurally rising.

### 7. Full-scale branded fitness centre / gym (ఫుల్-స్కేల్ జిమ్ · ఫిట్‌నెస్) — service 🏋️
- **Capex ₹30–50 L**: imported/branded equipment ₹18–30L, flooring/mirrors/AC, ~4000 sqft
  deposit + fit-out, (optional franchise fee ₹3–7L), working capital.
- **Unit economics**: membership **₹1,500–4,000/month**; 300–500 members → revenue
  **₹5–12L/month**; **net 25–40%** at good retention.
- **Payback ~24–36 months.**
- **Licences**: Udyam, Shops & Estab, trade, GST, fire/safety.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 high capex + **retention** + location; win on personal-training/nutrition
  upsell, community, group classes, and a specialisation. Fitness market doubling by 2030.

### 8. Mini flour / dal / rice mill (మినీ ఫ్లోర్ · పప్పు · రైస్ మిల్) — grain processing 🌾
- **Capex ₹25–50 L**: milling + cleaning + grading + packing machinery ₹12–28L, shed +
  3-phase power, godown, working capital (grain stock is heavy).
- **Unit economics**: milling margin is thin per kg — a **volume + branding** game; branded
  atta/dal/rice **gross 15–30%, net 8–15%**; steady staple demand. Grain stock = big WC.
- **Payback ~24–36 months.**
- **Licences**: **FSSAI (mandatory)**, Udyam, GST, factory/trade, pollution, (mandi licence
  for grain trade). PMEGP fits.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 grain price + working capital stuck in stock; win on a local brand,
  consistent quality, and B2B (kirana, mess, hostels) supply.

### 9. Rooftop solar EPC & installation company (రూఫ్‌టాప్ సోలార్ EPC) — green energy 🌞
- **Capex ₹25–45 L**: initial panel/inverter inventory + tools + installation vehicle +
  trained crew + certification/empanelment + working capital (projects need upfront material).
- **Unit economics**: a 5 kW residential project ≈ **₹2.5–3.5L revenue**, EPC **gross
  15–25%**; commercial/industrial rooftops are bigger; ride **PM Surya Ghar subsidy** demand.
- **Payback ~18–30 months** (project-flow driven).
- **Licences**: Udyam, GST, **DISCOM vendor empanelment**, electrical contractor licence,
  BIS/MNRE-approved components.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 project flow + working capital + execution quality; win on DISCOM
  empanelment, AMC contracts, and C&I clients. India's rooftop-solar push is a strong tailwind.

### 10. Readymade garment manufacturing unit (రెడీమేడ్ గార్మెంట్ యూనిట్) — apparel mfg 👕
- **Capex ₹30–50 L**: 25–35 sewing + specialised machines ₹10–20L, cutting table + finishing
  + packing, shed, fabric (raw material), working capital.
- **Unit economics**: job-work or own-label; **gross 20–35%, net 10–18%**; fabric ≈ 55–65%
  of cost; runs on **order volume + on-time delivery**. Contract manufacturing lowers risk.
- **Payback ~24–36 months.**
- **Licences**: Udyam, GST, factory/trade, (labour compliances at 20+ workers). PMEGP fits.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 labour management + order flow + fabric price; win on a niche
  (uniforms, kidswear, ethnic), buyer tie-ups, and quality/speed. India textile tailwind (PLI).

### 90-day start plan (any idea)
1. **Days 1–20** — pick the idea by the lens + your temperament; validate demand (talk to
   30 buyers / do a location study); draft a detailed project report (DPR) with real numbers.
2. **Days 21–45** — Udyam + GST + bank account; arrange money (own margin + term loan /
   PMEGP); finalise site/space; get machine + fit-out quotes.
3. **Days 46–75** — order machinery; apply for FSSAI / pollution / drug / DISCOM licences as
   needed; begin fit-out; hire and train the core team.
4. **Days 76–90** — trial production / soft launch; first customers or B2B orders; hold
   **6–9 months** working capital as a cushion. Ramp utilisation before adding capacity.

### Common mistakes (kill list)
- Spending all ₹50L on machinery/interiors, leaving **no working capital**.
- Modelling break-even at 100% utilisation — reality is a **slow ramp**; model 40–50%.
- Underestimating **team/labour** management at this scale.
- Wrong location (restaurant, gym, lab, service centre) — location is 50% of the outcome.
- Ignoring licences (FSSAI/pollution/drug/DISCOM) → fines, shutdown.
- Giving too much B2B credit → cash stuck, unit starves.
