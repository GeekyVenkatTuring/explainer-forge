## Title
Intraday Trading Explained From First Principles (Every Idea, With Something You Already Know)

## Description
Most intraday traders lose money — a SEBI study found 71% of individual intraday
traders in the equity cash segment made a net loss in FY23. So this isn't a tip-sheet.
It's the same repeatable framework, but taught from first principles: every idea is
explained with something you already know, then built up to the real mechanics.

We use two teaching methods under the hood — ADEPT (Analogy → Diagram → Example →
Plain-English → Technical term) and the Feynman technique (explain it simply, in
everyday words, before naming the jargon). So:

• Intraday = a rented bike you must return by evening (the "square-off")
• Delivery vs intraday = buying a home vs renting one (full price vs a deposit)
• The trading day = a city's rush hours (trade the open & close, skip the mid-day lull)
• Leverage = a magnifying glass — a 2% move looks like a 10% swing (capped ~5× by SEBI)
• Costs = a toll booth on every trip (~₹82 a round-trip; ~₹98k a year at 5 trades/day)
• Trend/bias = swimming with the river, not against it
• Levels = the floors and ceilings price bounces off
• VWAP = the day's "water level" that price floats around
• The setup (ORB) = a horse in the starting gate — wait for it to break out
• Don't chase = catch the bus at the next stop (the retest), not mid-sprint
• Volume = the crowd's roar that confirms a real move
• Stop-loss = a seatbelt you buckle before you drive
• Risk-reward = only take bets that pay 2-to-1
• Position sizing = a poker bankroll rule — never bet the farm (risk 1% a trade)
• Right hours = fish when the fish are biting (dawn & dusk, not noon)
• Psychology = your toughest opponent is in the mirror (avoid "tilt")

…finishing with one full example trade and a 60-second pre-trade checklist.

⚠ DISCLAIMER: This video is for EDUCATION ONLY and is NOT investment advice. It is
general information, not tailored to your situation, and mentions no specific stocks
or platforms. Trading is risky and most intraday traders lose money. Please consult
a SEBI-registered investment adviser before trading or investing. Figures quoted are
from public sources and current as of July 2026; rules, taxes and charges change.

Sources for the data cited: SEBI study on individual intraday traders (2024); SEBI
peak-margin framework; NSE market hours; broker charge schedules (STT, stamp duty,
GST, brokerage). All numeric examples are illustrative and computed on-screen.
