## A. Foundations (stable)
- Equity = ownership share in a company. Derivatives = instruments whose value derives from an
  underlying (stock/index/commodity). Three arenas: equities (cash), F&O (derivatives), commodities.
- India: NSE (1992), BSE (1875); regulator SEBI (1992). Depositories NSDL, CDSL. Commodities: MCX.
- Market hours: equities 9:15–15:30; MCX 9:00–23:30 (evening session for bullion/energy).

  pick the strongest 1–2 companies in it (leaders with moat).

## C. How to research a stock
Fundamental:
- Three statements: P&L (revenue, margins, net profit — check YoY), Balance Sheet (assets vs debt),
  Cash Flow (real cash from operations vs paper profit).
- Ratios: ROE (>15% good), ROCE, Debt/Equity (<1 safer), PE (vs sector avg), PB, PEG, dividend yield,
  interest coverage. Promoter holding (high & unpledged = good; pledge = red flag).
- Qualitative: moat (brand/network/cost/switching), management quality & governance, industry runway,
  capital-allocation history. Read the Annual Report + concall transcripts.
- Valuation: PE (price per ₹1 earnings), PEG (<1 attractive), DCF (advanced). "Great company ≠ great
  stock if overpriced."
Technical (for timing, not what-to-buy): trend, support/resistance, volume, moving averages
(50/200-DMA, "golden cross"), RSI, breakouts. Charts show the past — probabilistic, not prophecy.

## E. Equity investment strategies (define each + who it's for)
1. Buy & hold (long-term compounding) — beginners; time in market.
2. Value investing (buy below intrinsic value; Graham/Buffett; low PE/PB, margin of safety).
3. Growth investing (high earnings growth, higher PE tolerated).
4. GARP (growth at reasonable price; PEG < 1).
5. Dividend / income investing (steady payers, dividend yield; retirees).
6. Momentum / factor (Nifty 200 Momentum 30; buy strength; semi-annual rebalance; "momentum crashes").

8. Core-satellite (70–80% index core + 20–30% satellite factor/theme).
9. SIP / rupee-cost averaging (systematic, removes timing).
10. Contrarian (buy fear, sell greed).
11. Sector rotation (rotate into sectors with the macro tailwind).
12. Index/passive (own the market cheaply).

## F. Futures
- A future = a standardized, exchange-traded contract to buy/sell an underlying at a set price on a
  future date. Origin analogy: farmer–miller forward contract to lock price.
- Mechanics: traded in LOTS (Nifty lot 65 from Jan 2026; stock lots vary); pay MARGIN (~10–15% of
  contract value = leverage ~7–10x); daily MARK-TO-MARKET (P&L settled daily, margin calls); monthly
  EXPIRY (last Tue for Nifty). Nifty weekly expiry = Tuesday; Sensex = Thursday.
- Leverage cuts both ways: ₹1.5L margin on ₹15L position (10x); +5% index → +₹75k (+50% on margin);
  −5% → −₹75k (−50%).
- Futures strategies: (a) Hedging — protect a portfolio by shorting index futures. (b) Directional —
  long/short a view (high risk). (c) Calendar spread — long one expiry, short another. (d) Cash-futures
  arbitrage — exploit spot vs futures price gap (institutional, low risk).
- Players: hedgers (risk reduction — the real purpose), speculators (directional bets — where most
  retail loses), arbitrageurs (price-gap, algos).

## G. Options
- An option = a contract giving the RIGHT (not obligation) to buy (CALL) or sell (PUT) an underlying
  at a STRIKE price by EXPIRY, for a PREMIUM. Analogy: insurance (premium buyer = right; seller = obligation).
- Buyer: limited loss (premium), unlimited/large upside. Seller/writer: limited gain (premium),
  large/unlimited loss; needs big margin.
- Price = intrinsic value + time (extrinsic) value. Intrinsic: call = max(0, spot−strike); put =
  max(0, strike−spot). ITM/ATM/OTM.
- Greeks (verified via Option Alpha/Optiver): DELTA = ₹ change in option per 1-unit move in underlying
  (call 0..1, put −1..0). GAMMA = rate of change of delta. THETA = daily time decay (negative for
  buyers; "melting ice cube"). VEGA = sensitivity to 1% change in implied volatility. (RHO = rates,
  minor.) Delta/gamma ~ intrinsic side; theta/vega ~ extrinsic/time side.
- Options strategies (define payoff + when):
  Single-leg: Long Call (bullish, limited risk), Long Put (bearish), Short Call/Put (income, high risk).
  Income/hedge: Covered Call (own stock + sell call), Protective Put (own stock + buy put = insurance),
  Cash-Secured Put (sell put to buy lower).
  Spreads (defined risk): Bull Call Spread, Bear Put Spread, Bull Put (credit), Bear Call (credit).
  Volatility: Long Straddle (buy ATM call+put — big move either way), Long Strangle (cheaper, wider),
  Iron Condor (range-bound, sell OTM call+put spreads), Butterfly (pinpoint range).
- Time decay favors the SELLER; most options expire worthless → sellers (institutions with margin)
  win more often, but with tail risk.
- **F&O REALITY (SEBI, must state)**: FY25 study — 91% of individual F&O traders lost money; aggregate
  net loss ₹1.06 lakh crore; avg ~₹1.1 lakh/person. FY23 intraday — 71% lost. SEBI curbs 2024–25:
  fewer weekly expiries, bigger lots (~₹15–20L), full upfront premium.
- STT 2026: futures 0.05% (sell), options 0.15% of premium (sell). Options are for the educated,
  well-capitalized, disciplined — NOT beginners.

## H. Commodities
- Commodities trade on MCX (largest, SEBI-regulated). Categories: bullion (gold, silver), energy
  (crude oil, natural gas), base metals (copper, zinc, aluminium, lead), agri.
- Traded via FUTURES contracts. Lot examples (verify): Gold 1kg (main) + Gold Mini 100g; Silver;
  Crude Oil main 100 barrels + Mini 10 barrels. Margin small vs value (e.g., gold ~₹5–6k mini) =
  leverage. Hours 9:00–23:30 (tracks global markets).
- Drivers: gold/silver = safe-haven, USD, real rates, geopolitics, festival/wedding demand (India);
  crude = OPEC, geopolitics, demand; base metals = global growth/China.
- Uses: hedging (jewellers hedge gold, OMCs hedge crude), speculation, portfolio diversification
  (gold ~5–10% as a hedge — low correlation to equities). Also: Gold ETFs / Sovereign Gold Bonds as
  non-leveraged ways to own gold.
- Commodity strategies: trend-following, hedging, spread (crack spread crude, gold-silver ratio),
  seasonal. High leverage + 24hr global news = high risk.

## I. Risk management (applies to all)
- Position sizing: risk ≤1–2% of capital per trade. Always use stop-loss for leveraged/active.
- Diversify across sectors + asset classes. Don't mix investing capital with trading capital.
- Never use borrowed money / MTF leverage to chase returns (callback to 91% F&O loss).
- Match instrument to goal & skill: beginners → index SIP + quality equity; F&O/commodities only
  after education, small size, full rules. "Survival first, returns second."
