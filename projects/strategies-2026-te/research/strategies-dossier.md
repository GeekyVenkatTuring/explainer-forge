## 1. SEBI Mutual Fund Categorization & Rationalization (circular Feb 2026, EFFECTIVE 1 APR 2026)
Source: SEBI circular "Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes" (sebi.gov.in,
Feb 2026); INDmoney / Upstox learning centre explainers.
- **True-to-label equity minimums**: Large Cap min **80%** large-caps; Flexi Cap min **65%** equity;
  **Multi Cap min 25% each** in large/mid/small; Large & Mid min 35% each; Mid/Small min 65% in own cap.
- **Solution-Oriented category REMOVED** (Retirement Funds + Children's Funds). Existing schemes stop
  fresh subscriptions, merge into similar schemes. Reason: they were sold emotionally without
  structural backing.
- **Replaced by new "Life Cycle Funds"**: open-ended, target-maturity, **glide path** (high equity
  early → safer assets near maturity), tenures **5–30 years**.
- **Overlap cap**: Sectoral/Thematic schemes ≤ **50%** portfolio overlap with other equity schemes
  (except Large Cap); measured quarterly on daily values; 3-year window to comply.
- Investor impact: clearer names (true-to-label), less mis-selling, slightly lower/clearer costs.
- **Action for viewer**: re-check your fund's category vs its real holdings; don't rely on a
  "retirement/children" label; if you held a solution-oriented fund, see what it merged into;
  prefer true-to-label index/flexi + direct + growth.

## 2. Core–Satellite portfolio construction (evergreen framework, 2026 consensus)
Source: INDmoney core-satellite explainer; smallcase/Groww Nifty-50 guides.
- **Core (70–80%)**: low-cost broad index — Nifty 50 / Nifty Next 50 / Nifty 100 index funds.
  Stability, low cost, broad exposure. Many keep 30–50% of equity in Nifty 50 alone.
- **Satellite (20–30%)**: factor (momentum), mid/small-cap, thematic — for extra return, small size.
- **Passive AUM crossed ₹14 lakh crore by March 2026** (rising passive adoption).
- Sample split used on screen (illustrative, NOT advice): Nifty 50 50% · Next 50 20% · Momentum 20% · Mid/theme 10%.

## 3. Factor / Momentum index investing (2026 popular satellite)
Source: niftyindices.com Nifty200 Momentum 30 factsheet & whitepaper (2026); smallcase collections;
Value Research / Tickertape fund pages.
- **Nifty 200 Momentum 30**: top 30 of Nifty 200 by normalized momentum score (6M + 12M returns,
  volatility-adjusted); factor-tilt weights; **rebalanced semi-annually**.
- Funds: UTI / Motilal Oswal / ICICI Pru / Bandhan Nifty200 Momentum 30 Index Funds. UTI expense
  ratio ~**0.43%**; std dev ~**18.2** (higher than Nifty 50/200).
- **CAVEATS (must state)**: momentum is *aggressive*, prone to **"momentum crashes"** — sharp
  reversals, esp. during recoveries after big drawdowns; higher turnover; sector concentration.
  Satellite-sized only; not a core replacement.

## 4. Harvest the ₹1.25 lakh LTCG exemption + tax-loss harvesting (2026 tax regime)
Source: WealthOrbit / 1Finance / JM Financial / Tax2win 2026 guides (LTCG 12.5%, STCG 20% verified
in stock-market dossier).
- **Tax-GAIN harvesting**: each FY sell equity/equity-MF winners to book up to **₹1.25 lakh** LTCG
  (tax-free), then rebuy → resets cost of acquisition higher → saves 12.5% on that slab permanently.
- **Tax-LOSS harvesting**: sell losers to realise losses. **STCL offsets both** STCG & LTCG; LTCL
  offsets only LTCG. Unused losses carry forward 8 years.
- **India has NO strict wash-sale rule** — but wait ≥1 trading session or switch to a similar ETF/fund
  and document, to keep it clean.
- **Multiple entities** (self + spouse + HUF) can each use the exemption → up to **₹3.75 lakh**/yr tax-free.
- **Deadline**: before **31 March** of the financial year.

## 5. SIF — Specialized Investment Funds (new asset class, advanced)
Source: Groww / Zerodha Fund House / Tradebrains / Choice 2026 explainers.
- New SEBI asset class **between mutual funds and PMS**, live from **1 Apr 2025**.
- **Minimum ₹10 lakh** aggregate per PAN across strategies. Managers **NISM-certified**.
- Advanced strategies: **long-short**, sector rotation, dynamic allocation, derivatives.
- **Long-short ~76% of SIF assets**. AUM grew from **~₹2,010 cr (Oct 2025) → ~₹9,711 cr (Feb 2026)**.
- Limits: ≤10% in one listed company; ≤20% one debt issuer.
- **CAVEAT**: for HNIs / sophisticated investors only; ₹10L min; NOT for beginners; accessed via
  AMC/SIF platforms, not the core beginner apps yet.

## Brokerage implementation facts (2026)
Source: Sharenox / InvestingPro / Moneycontain broker comparisons; Zerodha/Groww product pages.
- **Groww**: cleanest MF interface, zero commission / zero AMC on direct MFs, easy SIP — best for
  beginners doing index/momentum SIPs.
- **Zerodha**: ecosystem — **Coin** (direct MFs), **smallcase** (one-click factor/momentum baskets in
  demat, rules-based auto-rebalance), **Varsity** (free education), **Console/Screener**. Best for
  core+satellite + smallcase momentum.
- **Upstox**: MF SIPs + low-cost trading; lost ~47,000 clients in Apr 2026 (SEBI F&O curbs + Groww/
  Zerodha pull). Fine for SIPs.
- **How to start an index/momentum SIP (all three)**: search the fund → pick **Direct** plan +
  **Growth** option → set SIP amount + date → approve UPI/e-mandate → auto-invests monthly.
- **MTF**: up to ~4X leverage on delivery on some brokers (Dhan 4X) — mention only as caution, not a
  strategy (leverage cuts both ways; callback to SEBI F&O 91%-loss studies).
- Direct + Growth is the default recommendation (lower cost, full compounding) — consistent with the
  main course.
