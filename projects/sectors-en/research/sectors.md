# Sector deep-dives — verified facts (2026-07-21)

## Finance / Banking (sources: IndMoney, Kotak Sec, mStock, Winvesta)
- NIM 3–4% healthy (HDFC ~3.35%, ICICI ~4.3%, Kotak ~4.54% Q3FY26).
- GNPA/NNPA: <1% strong, >~2% worry (<0.5% excellent).
- CASA: higher = cheaper funding (Kotak ~50%).
- ROA: >1% good, >1.5% excellent. ROE, provision coverage, cost-to-income (lower better).
- CAR: RBI min ~11.5%; strong banks 17–22%+ (HDFC 19.9%, ICICI 17.34%, Kotak 22.6%).
- Valuation: banks on **P/B not P/E**. HDFC ~3x book; SBI ~1.5–2x (PSU discount).
- Weight ~35–40% of Nifty; Bank Nifty most liquid derivative; RBI-driven.

- Budget 2026-27: ₹6.81 lakh cr defence (+13% YoY); capital outlay ₹2.19 lakh cr (+21%).

- Key metric = ORDER BOOK & book-to-bill (BEL ~₹76,000 cr ≈ 4.5 yrs visibility).
- Watch execution, EBITDA margin, working capital/cash (long cycles lock cash).
- Nifty India Defence: PE ~57 vs 3-yr median ~52 (slightly rich). Weights HAL ~24.35%,
  Solar Industries ~13.77%, Mazagon Dock ~8.22%, Bharat Forge ~7.45% (May 2026). Momentum + event-driven, volatile.

## Technology / IT (sources: ICICI Direct, Samco, Tickertape, sector-rotation pieces)
- Export-driven (US/EU); USD/INR sensitive (weak ₹ helps); US client budgets & results = catalyst.
- Metrics: constant-currency revenue growth, deal TCV/pipeline, EBIT margin (~20%+ large-cap), attrition.
- 2026 context: Nifty IT down ~25% YTD → "value zone"; stock selection > sector exposure.
- AI = both threat (automation) and opportunity (new deals) → winners vs losers.
- Cash returners (dividends + buybacks). Partial defensive.

## Cross-sector technicals (sources: Equilytics, BusinessToday sector-rotation)
- Track the sector index (Bank Nifty / Nifty India Defence / Nifty IT); relative strength vs Nifty.
- Sector rotation: capital moves between sectors by cycle; >70% breadth + positive index = genuine
  rotation (not 1–2 large-caps). MAs/RSI on the sector index. Banking leads → Nifty follows.
