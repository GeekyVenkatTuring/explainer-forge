## Title
Intraday Trading Explained: A Framework You Can Actually Follow (Beginner → Pro)

## Description
Most intraday traders lose money — a SEBI study found 71% of individual intraday
traders in the equity cash segment made a net loss in FY23. This video is not a
tip-sheet. It's one repeatable framework: what intraday trading really is, what it
costs, how leverage works, and a step-by-step way to find, enter, size, and manage
a trade with rules — the same discipline the profitable minority use.

We cover the mechanics (same-day square-off, margin, costs, the trading-day rhythm),
then the tips that actually matter: trade with the trend, mark your levels, use VWAP,
wait for a setup (opening range breakout), don't chase, confirm with volume, always
use a stop-loss, demand 1:2 risk-reward, size by the 1% rule, trade the right hours,
and master your psychology — finishing with a full example trade and a pre-trade
checklist.

⚠ DISCLAIMER: This video is for EDUCATION ONLY and is NOT investment advice. It is
general information, not tailored to your situation, and mentions no specific stocks
or platforms. Trading is risky and most intraday traders lose money. Please consult
a SEBI-registered investment adviser before trading or investing. Figures quoted are
from public sources and current as of July 2026; rules, taxes and charges change.

Sources for the data cited: SEBI study on individual intraday traders (2024); SEBI
peak-margin framework; NSE market hours; broker charge schedules (STT, stamp duty,
GST, brokerage). All numeric examples are illustrative and computed on-screen.
