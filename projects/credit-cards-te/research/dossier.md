## 1. Market context (hook material)
- ~**114.9 million** credit cards in circulation in India (Nov 2025), up from ~111M mid-2025. [Business Standard, RBI data]
- Total credit-card **outstanding ≈ ₹2.88 lakh crore** (Aug 2025), up from ₹340 crore a decade earlier (~84,000% in 10 yrs). [Outlook India — VERIFIED]
- Monthly credit-card **spends hit a festive record ₹2.17 trillion in Sep 2025**, then moderated to ₹1.89 trillion (Nov 2025). Do NOT call ₹2.17T "current" — it's the Sept festive peak. [Business Standard — VERIFIED]
- Private banks hold **roughly 70%+** of the card market (soften — exact 70.8% not confirmed from primary RBI release). [VERIFIED direction only]
- Takeaway for hook: cards are exploding in India, but so is outstanding debt — power tool + trap.

## 2. What a credit card is / how it works
- **Billing cycle**: 28–31 days; bank generates a monthly statement on the statement date. [Multiple]
- **Grace / interest-free period**: 20–50 days between statement date and due date; due date usually 18–21 days after statement. **Only applies if you paid last month's bill in FULL** — carry even ₹100 and the grace period vanishes. [InvestingPro, IndiaLends]
- **Timing trick**: buying the day after the statement date → up to ~48 interest-free days; buying just before statement → ~19 days.
- **Interest rate**: ~**2.5–3.75% per month**, i.e. roughly **30–45% per annum**. Safe phrasing: "up to ~3.75%/month, about 45% a year." ICICI ~3.75%/mo (45% p.a.). [ICICI, Paisabazaar — VERIFIED]
- **Minimum amount due (MAD)**: usually **5% of the total** (or ₹200, whichever higher). Paying only MAD keeps account "current" but interest accrues on the other 95% + grace period lost on new spends. [CreditMitra, 1Finance]

## 3. RBI rules (2022 Master Direction on Credit & Debit Cards + amendments)
- **OTP-based consent** required if a card isn't activated within 30 days of issuance. [BusinessToday]
- **Card-network choice**: issuers can't lock into one network; must offer multiple networks — applies to issuers with ≥10 lakh active cards. [RBI]
- **No negative amortization**: MAD terms must be set so the balance doesn't perpetually grow; issuers must **prominently warn** about paying only MAD. [RBI Master Circular]
- **"Past due" reporting** only after >3 days overdue; late fees only on the amount outstanding after due date, not the whole bill. [RBI amendment]
- **Card closure** must be honoured within **7 working days**, else **₹500/day penalty** to the customer. [RBI]
- **Key Fact Statement (KFS)**: one-page summary of interest/charges must accompany the application. [RBI]

## 4. How to benefit (right usage)
- **Pay in full, every time** — the single most important habit; avoids all interest, builds score. [1Finance, HDFC]
- **Rewards / cashback**: Cashback SBI Card — 5% online (cap ₹5,000/mo), 1% offline; Amazon Pay ICICI — up to 5% for Prime, lifetime-free; HDFC Millennia — 5% on Amazon/Flipkart/Myntra; Axis ACE — 5% on bill pay via Google Pay. [Paisabazaar, CardExpert] **[VERIFY current rates]**
- **Credit score (CIBIL)**: 300–900 by TransUnion CIBIL (RBI-licensed); >750 excellent. Utilization = **one of the largest factors (~25–30%)**, second to payment history. Keep utilization **below 30%**. [HDFC, BankBazaar — VERIFIED]
- **No-cost EMI reality**: not truly free — **18% GST on the interest component** + processing fee (₹99–₹500) + GST on that; cashback often forfeited. A ₹30,000 no-cost EMI can really cost ₹30,810+. [ClearTax, myPaisaa] **[VERIFY GST mechanics]**
- **Forex markup**: typically **2–3.5%** (incl. GST); some cards ~0.99–1% (e.g. MakeMyTrip ICICI, OneCard 1%); zero-forex options: Niyo, IDFC FIRST WOW!, Scapia. [Wise, Paisabazaar] **[VERIFY specific cards]**

## 5. Wrong usage / the debt trap
- **Minimum-due trap**: ₹50,000 at 42% p.a. paying only 5% MAD → most goes to interest; can take **30+ years** to clear, paying far more than borrowed. [1Finance, DebtZero]
- **Cash withdrawal**: no interest-free period — interest from day one + cash advance fee (₹100–₹300 or 2.5–3%). [Wise, Paisabazaar]
- **Overspending psychology**: credit "feels free," people spend more than with cash/UPI.
- **Escape routes**: convert outstanding to EMI (12–18% ≪ 42%); personal-loan consolidation (10–16%); stop using the card; pay full. [DebtZero, 1Finance]

## 6. Specific cards (Indian market)
- **SBI** — Cashback SBI Card (5% online), SimplyCLICK, SBI Card ELITE. Public-sector leader.
- **HDFC** — Millennia (5% e-com), MoneyBack, Regalia Gold, Infinia (invite-only, ~3.33% base up to 33.33% on SmartBuy). Largest private issuer.
- **ICICI** — Amazon Pay ICICI (lifetime-free, 5% for Prime), Platinum Chip, MakeMyTrip (0.99% forex).
- **Axis** — ACE (5% bill pay via GPay), Flipkart Axis, Magnus (premium).
- **OneCard** (fintech, issued w/ partner banks incl. IDFC FIRST, BOB, SBM, Federal) — **metal, lifetime-free**, app-first, 5x points on top-2 categories monthly, points never expire, **1% forex**, 3.75%/mo interest on revolve. 5-min digital onboarding. Positioned for beginners. [Paisabazaar, CardInsider, Wise]
- **Uni Card** — cautionary/startup story: launched "Uni Pay 1/3rd" (split bill in 3). **Suspended card services Aug 2022** after RBI (June 2022) barred non-bank PPI issuers from loading credit lines onto PPIs. Since **pivoted to "Uni GoldX"** (now issued as BOBCARD / YES Bank variant): lifetime-free, ~5% back on brands (Amazon/Swiggy/Flipkart), ~7% on travel via the Uni app, 1% on UPI/offline — redeemable as 24K digital gold; zero forex, no annual fee. (Series A Sep 2024, ~123 staff — treat as color, not on-screen stat.) Good lesson on regulation + startup risk. [Business Standard, Inc42, Paisabazaar — VERIFIED]

## 7. Community / expert consensus (beginner mistakes)
- Treating the limit as "free money"; missing due dates; carrying balances; overspending vs cash; not tracking utilization. Advice: start with a card fitting your profile, spend only what you can repay, pay full & on time, keep utilization <30%. [r/CreditCardsIndia consensus]
