# Chicken Pakodi Business — Hyderabad — Research Dossier
Compiled 2026-08-17. All ₹ figures are INDICATIVE RANGES for an education video (not
financial advice). Triangulated from restaurant listings, IndiaMART equipment listings,
FSSAI/GHMC guides, and India street-food economics. Verify locally before investing.

## 1. Menu & pricing benchmark (Hyderabad chicken pakoda)
Chicken pakoda ("chicken pakodi") is a signature Hyderabad snack — sold both by
dedicated joints and inside biryani restaurants as a starter.

| Place / type | What it's known for | Indicative price |
|---|---|---|
| A1 Chicken Pakoda Point (Dilsukhnagar/Kothapet) | THE iconic dedicated chicken-pakoda joint | ~₹150–180 / plate; ₹350 for two |
| Quality Chicken Pakoda (Kukatpally) | popular neighbourhood specialist | ~₹150–200 / plate |
| Five Star Chicken (QSR chain) | branded chicken pakora | ~₹100 / portion |
| Biryani restaurants (Bawarchi, Cafe Bahar, Shah Ghouse) | pakoda as a starter alongside biryani | ~₹200–280 / plate (sit-down) |

**Takeaways:** dedicated joints win on FOCUS + price (₹150–180 a 250g plate). Sit-down
restaurants charge ₹200–280 but pakoda is a side, not the hero. Positioning gap = a
FOCUSED chicken-pakodi kiosk near footfall, ₹150–180 plate, consistent taste.
Portion norm: ~250g fried per "plate"; also sold by weight (₹500–700 / kg).

## 2. Location & footfall (Hyderabad)
High-footfall food-stall zones (evening street-food crowds, students, techies, families):
- **Dilsukhnagar / Kothapet** — dense, student + family footfall; A1 proves demand here.
- **Ameerpet** — coaching-hub students, cheap-snack culture, all-day footfall.
- **KPHB / Kukatpally (IDL Lake food street)** — 15–20 stalls, strong evening crowd.
- **Madhapur / HITEC City (300m night food street)** — techies, late-night, higher spend.
- **Gachibowli / Financial District** — offices, young earners, weekend demand.
- **Secunderabad (Paradise/Patny), Begumpet** — mixed transit + office footfall.
- Near **colleges, bus stops, metro exits, tech-park gates, cinema/market clusters**.

What makes a spot good: evening/night footfall, students + families + office-goers,
parking/standing room, visible frontage, low direct chicken-pakoda competition,
affordable rent vs footfall. Traffic ≠ footfall — you want people who STOP and walk,
not just vehicles passing. Scout a spot at 6–9 PM before signing.

## 3. Setup, licenses & investment (kiosk / small outlet)
Formats: (a) branded push-cart/kiosk ₹1.5–4L, (b) small 150–300 sqft takeaway
₹4–10L, (c) cloud/Swiggy-Zomato-first ₹2–5L.

Equipment (indicative):
- Stainless-steel chicken-pakodi counter/cart: **~₹32,000** (IndiaMART, Hyderabad).
- Deep fryer / big kadai + burner + LPG: ₹15,000–1,00,000 (size dependent).
- Prep: fridge/freezer ₹25,000–60,000, work tables, vessels, weighing scale.
- Signage, branding, packaging, POS/QR: ₹20,000–60,000.

Licenses:
- **FSSAI**: Basic Registration ₹100/yr if turnover < ₹12L; State License
  ₹2,000–7,500/yr for ₹12L–20Cr. FSSAI number must be displayed. MANDATORY.
- **GST**: register once turnover crosses ₹40L (goods) / ₹20L (services).
- **GHMC trade license** required to sell food within GHMC limits (+ Fire NOC for
  larger spaces). Operating without can attract heavy penalties.
- Shop & Establishment registration; Udyam (MSME) optional but useful.

Indicative total to start a focused kiosk: **≈ ₹3–6 lakh** (cart+fryer+fridge+deposit+
branding+working capital). Small takeaway outlet: ₹6–10L.

## 4. Unit economics (per ~250g boneless plate, indicative)
Cost side (chicken is the biggest cost — pakoda is a VOLUME game, not a fat-margin one):
- Boneless chicken raw ~180–200g @ ₹320–360/kg → ₹65–90
- Besan + rice flour + corn flour + spices/egg → ₹8–14
- Cooking oil (absorbed) → ₹12–20
- Onion, chutney, packaging, gas → ₹12–18
- **Cost per plate ≈ ₹100–135**
Sell at **₹160–180** → **gross ₹45–70 / plate (~30–40%)**.

Monthly (kiosk, ~120–180 plates/day):
- Revenue ≈ ₹6–10L/month at good footfall
- Food cost 55–65%, rent+labour+power+gas 15–25%
- **Net margin ≈ 12–18%** → ₹80,000–1,80,000/month at a strong location
- **Payback ≈ 8–18 months** for a ₹3–6L kiosk if footfall holds.
Reality: margins swing with CHICKEN RATE (broiler ₹115–135/kg live can spike). Protect
margin with fixed portion weight, batch frying, low wastage, and a tight menu.

## 5. Recipe & operations (quality is the moat)
Recipe spine (restaurant-style): boneless chicken cut small → marinate (ginger-garlic,
chilli, curd/egg white "velveting", garam masala) 30–60 min → coat besan + rice/corn
flour (rice flour = crispiness) → deep-fry 165–175°C till golden → toss with curry
leaves, fried green chilli, chaat masala. Serve hot with onion + mint chutney.
Ops: same taste every time (standard recipe card + weighed portions), fresh oil cycle,
FIFO cold chain, hygiene/gloves (FSSAI), fast serve at peak (pre-marinate in batches),
Swiggy/Zomato + QR + WhatsApp for repeat, combos (pakodi + soft drink / rumali).

## 6. Risks
Chicken-rate volatility · oil cost · thin margins if portion not controlled · one bad
hygiene/taste day kills reviews · location dependence · competition · staff/attrition ·
festival/seasonal demand swings · monsoon footfall dips.
