## Description
This week, both the US and China opened new IPOs — but they could not look more different.
In America: a cluster of small deals, mostly biotech, plus a SPAC. In China: one or two
giants in chips and hard-tech, including a record-size Shanghai STAR Market listing and a
multi-billion-dollar Hong Kong deal. One combined, plain-English walkthrough of both.

We cover:
• What an IPO actually is (in one breath)
• The American way — this week's Nasdaq slate, the S-1 → SEC → book-build process, and what a SPAC is
• The Chinese way — the STAR Market vs ChiNext vs Hong Kong, CSRC sign-off, strategic placement, and the chip-sovereignty theme
• The side-by-side contrast: size, sectors, what drives each market, and who can buy
• What it means for you: ADRs vs Hong Kong access, VIE / dual-class / lock-up risks, and where to read the filings

Real IPOs referenced this week (names & businesses are the stable facts; dollar/yuan figures
are approximate and clearly labelled — dates and sizes are estimates that can change):
– US (Nasdaq): Attovia Therapeutics (ATTO), Braveheart Bio (BRVE), Vogenx (VOGX), OceanLight (OCLT, a SPAC)
– China: CXMT / ChangXin Memory (Shanghai STAR Market, DRAM chips), Zhongji Innolight (Hong Kong, optical modules)

⚠️ Education only — NOT investment advice, not a buy/sell recommendation, and not a
prediction of how any of these shares will trade. Read the official prospectus and consult
a licensed financial advisor before investing.
