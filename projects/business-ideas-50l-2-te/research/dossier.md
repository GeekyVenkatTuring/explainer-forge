# Business Ideas Under ₹50 Lakh — Volume 2 — Dossier (compiled 2026-08-07)

Second in the ₹50-lakh series (the bigger tier — machinery, a team, a real location).
Volume 1 covered: family restaurant, plastic/PVC mfg, modular furniture, frozen food,
car service, diagnostic lab, gym, grain mill, solar EPC, garment unit. **Volume 2 is 10
fresh ideas**, none overlapping Vol 1 or the under-₹25-lakh series.

All figures are **indicative industry ranges** (2025–26), shown on screen as RANGES, never
promises. Real cost varies by city, scale, vendor, automation, and owned-vs-leased space.
**Educational only — NOT financial / investment advice.** Verify with local vendors, a CA,
and current scheme rules before investing.

Audience: an owner in AP/TS with **₹25–50 lakh** (savings + a bank/PMEGP/term loan).
Language: Telugu + English (Tanglish); brand names, ₹ amounts, %, numbers stay Latin.

## Part 1 — The lens (same bigger-tier rules as Vol 1)

1. **Capex vs working capital** — bigger machine bills, but keep **6–9 months** of runway.
2. **Utilisation / capacity** — a hall, hotel, press, or fleet only earns when it runs full;
   model break-even at **40–50%** utilisation, not 100%.
3. **Payback** — longer here: typically **18–36 months**.
4. **Team & systems** — you hire 4–20 people; hiring, training, SOPs become the job.
5. **Moat & risk** — location + brand + B2B contracts + licences protect you; idle capacity
   or a wrong location burns cash fast.
- Common ground: Udyam (free), GST, bank term loan / CGTMSE cover, **PMEGP** margin-money
  subsidy 15–35% (manufacturing ideas), plus idea-specific licences (FSSAI, fire, drug-
  cosmetic, RTO, pollution).

## Part 2 — The 10 ideas (indicative, ₹25–50 L)

Legend: 🟢 easy/low · 🟡 medium · 🔴 hard/high.

### 1. Budget hotel / service apartments (బడ్జెట్ హోటల్ · సర్వీస్ అపార్ట్‌మెంట్స్) — hospitality 🏨
- **Capex ₹30–50 L** (leased building, 20–30 rooms): room furniture + fit-out ₹18–25L,
  AC/linen/amenities, reception + billing + branding, deposit + working capital.
- **Economics**: ARR **₹1,200–2,500/room**; occupancy **60–70%** at a good location; **net
  25–40%**; revenue **₹5–12L/month**. **Payback ~24–36 months.**
- **Licences**: trade, fire NOC, police/tourism registration, GST, FSSAI (if food).
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 location + ratings + OTA commission; win on direct + corporate bookings,
  clean rooms, repeat guests. Rides travel/tourism + business-travel growth.

### 2. Banquet / function hall (బ్యాంక్వెట్ · ఫంక్షన్ హాల్) — events 🎉
- **Capex ₹25–45 L** (leased 5,000+ sqft + parking): interiors + AC + lighting ₹12–18L,
  chairs/tables/stage, sound + decor, deposit + working capital.
- **Economics**: **₹50k–3L per event**; 8–15 events/month in season; **net 25–40%**; highly
  **seasonal** (wedding/festival). **Payback ~24–36 months.**
- **Licences**: trade, fire, sound/pollution, GST.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 location + parking + reputation; seasonal demand; win on packages,
  catering/decor tie-ups, and off-season corporate/events.

### 3. Commercial bakery (కమర్షియల్ బేకరీ) — bread · rusk · cakes 🍞
- **Capex ₹25–45 L**: deck/rotary ovens + mixers + proofer ₹15–22L, packing + cooling,
  shed, working capital.
- **Economics**: **gross 35–50%, net 12–20%**; B2B supply to shops/hotels + branded retail;
  a **volume** game. **Payback ~24–36 months.**
- **Licences**: **FSSAI (mandatory)**, Udyam, GST, factory/trade, pollution.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 maida/butter price + shelf life; win on a hero product (bread/rusk),
  a distribution route, and B2B (kirana, hotels, canteens).

### 4. Aluminium & uPVC fabrication (అల్యూమినియం · uPVC ఫ్యాబ్రికేషన్) — windows/doors 🪟
- **Capex ₹25–45 L**: cutting/crimping/welding machines ₹12–18L, tools + workshop, profile
  + glass + hardware stock, working capital.
- **Economics**: priced per sqft; **gross 25–40%, net 15–22%**; project-based (builders/
  homes). **Payback ~18–30 months.**
- **Licences**: Udyam, GST, trade, pollution (dust).
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 skilled fabricators + aluminium price; win on quality, design, on-time
  install, and builder/architect tie-ups. uPVC replacing wood/steel is the tailwind.

### 5. EV 2/3-wheeler dealership & service (EV టూ · త్రీ-వీలర్ డీలర్‌షిప్) — auto retail 🛺
- **Capex ₹30–50 L**: showroom fit-out + first vehicle inventory ₹18–25L, service tools +
  spares, charging, deposit + working capital.
- **Economics**: vehicle margin **8–12%** + service/spares **20–35%** + finance/insurance
  commission; **volume-driven**, revenue **₹8–20L/month**. **Payback ~24–36 months.**
- **Licences**: OEM dealership tie-up, RTO, Udyam, GST, trade.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 inventory working capital + thin vehicle margin + fast-changing tech;
  win on service/AMC, finance commission, and location. EV adoption + subsidy tailwind.

### 6. Packaged juice / beverage plant (ప్యాకేజ్డ్ జ్యూస్ · బెవరేజ్) — food processing 🥤
- **Capex ₹30–50 L**: processing (pulper/mixer/pasteuriser) + filling/bottling ₹18–28L,
  cold storage/chiller, bottles/packaging, FSSAI-grade setup, working capital.
- **Economics**: **gross 30–45%, net 12–20%**; branded juice/beverage; distribution + shelf
  is the job. **Payback ~24–36 months.**
- **Licences**: **FSSAI + BIS**, Udyam, GST, pollution.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 shelf life + cold chain + big brands; win on a niche flavour, local
  distribution, and HORECA/retail tie-ups. Packaged/health-beverage tailwind.

### 7. Commercial printing press (కమర్షియల్ ప్రింటింగ్ ప్రెస్) — offset + digital 🖨️
- **Capex ₹30–50 L**: offset/digital press ₹18–28L, cutting + binding + lamination, design
  station + software, shed, working capital.
- **Economics**: **gross 30–45%, net 15–22%**; B2B (books, boxes, labels, marketing);
  **order-flow** driven. **Payback ~24–36 months.**
- **Licences**: Udyam, GST, factory/trade, pollution.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 digital shift + paper price; win on a packaging/label niche, quality,
  speed, and steady B2B contracts. Packaging + print-on-demand tailwind.

### 8. Coaching institute (కోచింగ్ ఇన్‌స్టిట్యూట్) — competitive exams / multi-subject 🏫
- **Capex ₹25–45 L**: classrooms + furniture + AC + digital boards ₹14–20L, branding +
  admissions, deposit + faculty advance, working capital.
- **Economics**: fee **₹15k–60k/course/student**; 200–500 students; **net 25–40%** when
  batches fill; **seasonal admissions**. **Payback ~18–30 months.**
- **Licences**: Shops & Estab, trade, fire, GST.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 faculty retention + enrolment; win on a niche (NEET/EAMCET/banking/
  spoken English), results, demos, and word-of-mouth + an online hybrid.

### 9. Logistics & mini transport fleet (లాజిస్టిక్స్ · మినీ ట్రాన్స్‌పోర్ట్ ఫ్లీట్) — services 🚚
- **Capex ₹25–50 L**: 3–5 commercial vehicles (owned/down-payment) ₹20–30L, GPS/telematics,
  office, working capital.
- **Economics**: freight per trip/km; **net 12–20%**; contracts with e-com/FMCG/
  manufacturers; **utilisation** decides it. **Payback ~24–36 months.**
- **Licences**: transport permits, insurance, GST, Udyam.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 diesel + driver management + idle trucks; win on B2B freight contracts,
  reliability, and high utilisation. E-commerce + logistics growth tailwind.

### 10. Cosmetics / soap / personal-care unit (కాస్మెటిక్స్ · సబ్బు · పర్సనల్-కేర్) — light mfg 🧴
- **Capex ₹25–45 L**: mixing/filling machines ₹12–18L, moulds + packaging line, raw
  material + QC lab, FSSAI/BIS/cosmetic licence, working capital.
- **Economics**: **gross 30–50%, net 15–25%**; branded soap/handwash/cosmetics via D2C +
  retail + private-label. **Payback ~24–36 months.**
- **Licences**: **cosmetic manufacturing licence (Drugs & Cosmetics)**, Udyam, GST, BIS
  (some products), pollution.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 big brands + regulatory + marketing; win on a niche (herbal/handmade/
  natural), private-label supply, and D2C. Personal-care + D2C boom tailwind.

### 90-day start plan (any idea)
1. **Days 1–20** — pick by the lens + temperament; validate demand (talk to 30 buyers /
   location study); draft a detailed project report (DPR).
2. **Days 21–45** — Udyam + GST + bank account; arrange money (margin + term loan / PMEGP);
   finalise site; get machine + fit-out quotes.
3. **Days 46–75** — order machinery; apply licences (FSSAI / fire / cosmetic / RTO /
   pollution); fit-out; hire and train the core team.
4. **Days 76–90** — trial / soft launch; first customers or B2B orders; keep **6–9 months**
   working capital. Ramp utilisation before adding capacity.

### Common mistakes (kill list)
- All money on machinery/fit-out, **no working capital**.
- Modelling break-even at 100% utilisation — reality is a **slow ramp** (40–50%).
- Underestimating **team/labour** at this scale.
- Wrong location (hotel, hall, coaching, dealership) — location is half the outcome.
- Ignoring licences → fines, shutdown.
- Too much B2B credit → cash stuck, unit starves.
