# Lead-Acid Battery Recycling — Fundamentals Dossier (verified 2026-07-31)

All figures are **indicative industry ranges** cross-checked across ≥3 vendor/trade/regulatory
sources. Costs vary by capacity, vendor, and location — presented on screen as ranges, never
promises. Regulatory framework (BWMR 2022 / EPR / CPCB / SPCB) is stable and authoritative.

## Regulation (authoritative)
- **Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022 (BWMR 2022)** — replaced Batteries (M&H) Rules 2001;
  covers all battery chemistries; built on **EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility)**.
- **CPCB** centralized EPR portal (eprbattery.cpcb.gov.in) — recyclers/refurbishers/producers
  register. Recyclers generate & sell **EPR certificates** to producers (a real 2nd revenue line).
- **SPCB**: **TSPCB** (Telangana) / **APPCB** (Andhra) issue **CTE** (Consent to Establish) and
  **CTO** (Consent to Operate).
- **Hazardous & Other Wastes (M&TM) Rules, 2016** — HW authorization (filed with CTE).
- Also: Factory Licence (Factories Act 1948), Fire NOC, GST, BIS for ingots (IS 27 / IS 13345).
- **Cautionary tale (real):** APPCB ordered Amara Raja's AP plants (Karakambadi, Nunegundlapalli,
  Chittoor dist.) to shut over air pollution & high blood-lead in workers/nearby residents.
  Pollution compliance is EXISTENTIAL in this business. (Amara Raja also now runs recycling.)

## Process stages
1. Intake & segregation (weigh, log on EPR portal)
2. Battery breaking (drain H2SO4, separate lead grids / paste / PP) — manual vs hydro-separation
3. Smelting — **rotary furnace 1000–1200°C** → crude/bullion lead (+ slag/dross waste)
4. Refining (kettle) → 99.5–99.97% purity; alloying (Pb-Sb, Pb-Ca)
5. Acid recovery (lime neutralise or battery-grade H2SO4)
6. PP processing (wash, granulate) — by-product

## Machinery
Battery breaker · PP separator / hydro-separator · rotary furnace · refining kettle · alloying
furnace · ingot casting line · ETP (effluent) · **baghouse/bag filter · venturi scrubber · ID fan +
stack** · weighbridge · PPE/fire.
Indian makers: Indotherm (Ahmedabad), Faridabad Furnace, Kahlon Engineers (Ludhiana),
Eros Environmental, Madhusudan Furnaces.

## Investment — 2 TPD (~600 TPA) plant, indicative all-in ₹65 L – 1.1 cr
- Machinery (breaker, furnace, kettle): ₹40–80 L (used ~₹60 L midpoint)
- Site prep + ETP: ₹15–25 L (~₹20 L)
- Land: ₹15–50 L (~₹30 L; less in TSIIC/APIIC estate)
- Licences + pre-operative: ₹3–8 L + ₹2–5 L (~₹8 L)
- **Working capital (90-day):** ₹25–45 L (~₹35 L) — lead is costly per kg, cash cycle is the constraint
- Automatic plant (hydro-sep + full pollution control): **₹1.5 cr → several crore**
- Space: 9,000–14,000 sq ft; 3-phase 250–500 kVA; water 15–25 KLD; gas/LPG; notified industrial estate.

## Unit economics (per kg of ULAB input) — reconciled & honest
- ULAB scrap buy: **₹60–85/kg** (LME-linked; 2026 regional: Delhi ₹71, Akola ₹72, Rourkela ₹60)
- Recovered lead ingot sells: **₹150–185/kg**; recovery ~92–96% of the battery's lead content
- **You get ~0.6 kg lead per 1 kg battery** (rest = acid, plastic, separators) — so net ≠ 165−75.
- Honest per-kg model: cost = scrap ₹75 + processing ₹29 = **₹104**; revenue = lead ₹102 + PP ₹4 +
  EPR ₹6 = **₹112**; **net ≈ ₹8/kg (range ₹6–11)**. Gross margin 20–28%, net 7–13%.
- PP granules: 5–7% of weight @ ₹55–85/kg. EPR certificate: ₹4–12/kg of capacity.
- 2 TPD annual net operating profit: ₹36–66 L (before finance). **Payback 30–48 months.**
