  Canva Magic (images); CapCut AI, Opus Clip, HeyGen, ElevenLabs (video/voice);
  Surfer/Jasper (SEO content); n8n/Zapier (automation).

## Client acquisition / running the agency
- Niche down (clinics, real estate, restaurants, D2C, coaches) → portfolio → referrals.
- First clients: 1–2 free/cheap case studies → results → testimonials → outbound (DMs,
  local walk-ins, cold email) + inbound (own reels/SEO).
- Pricing models: per-project, monthly retainer (best), performance/rev-share.
- Retention = the game: reporting, communication, results. Churn is the #1 risk.
- Team as you scale: freelancers/VAs for editing/design; you stay on strategy+sales.
