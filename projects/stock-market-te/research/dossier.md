## Market structure (stable facts, common knowledge — low risk)
- BSE founded 1875 (Asia's oldest exchange); NSE founded 1992, electronic trading from 1994.
- SEBI: statutory market regulator since 1992 (SEBI Act). Investor complaints: SCORES portal.
- Sensex = 30 large BSE companies, base 1978-79 = 100. Nifty 50 = 50 large NSE companies,
  base Nov 1995 = 1000.
- Market hours: pre-open 9:00–9:15, normal session 9:15–15:30, Mon–Fri.
- Two depositories: NSDL and CDSL. Trading needs: bank a/c + trading a/c + demat a/c;
  KYC = PAN (mandatory) + Aadhaar + bank proof.

## Trading-risk studies (SEBI — the backbone of the caution chapters)
- **F&O**: SEBI study (Jul 2025, FY25 data): **91% of individual F&O traders lost money in
  FY25**; aggregate net loss **₹1.06 lakh crore** (up 41% from ₹74,812 cr in FY24); average
  loss per person ≈ **₹1.1 lakh**. Earlier study (Sep 2024): 93% lost over FY22–FY24,
  aggregate > ₹1.8 lakh crore. Sources: SEBI press releases; Business Standard Jul 2025.
- **Intraday (equity cash)**: SEBI study (Jul 2024, FY23 data): **7 out of 10 (71%) individual
  intraday traders made net losses**; 80% loss rate for very frequent traders (>500
  trades/yr); loss-makers additionally spent 57% of their losses as trading costs.
  Source: SEBI press release Jul 2024.

## Taxes & charges (effective FY as noted — say "as of 2026" in narration)
- **STCG** (equity, held ≤ 12 months, STT paid): **20%** (Sec 111A; raised from 15% on
  23 Jul 2024). **LTCG** (held > 12 months): **12.5%** on gains above **₹1.25 lakh/yr**
  exemption (raised from 10%/₹1L in Budget 2024). No indexation for listed equity.
- **STT (effective Apr 1 2026, Budget 2026 hike)**: delivery equity 0.1% (buy AND sell);
  intraday equity 0.025% (sell side); **futures 0.05% (sell)**; **options 0.15% of premium
  (sell)**. (Pre-Apr-2026 rates were futures 0.02%, options 0.1% — use the NEW rates.)
- Other charges: brokerage (discount brokers ₹0 delivery / ~₹20 per order intraday-F&O),
  exchange transaction charges, SEBI turnover fee, stamp duty, GST 18% on brokerage+charges,
  DP charges on sell. (Present as a category list, not exact bps — they change.)
- Equity MF taxation = same STCG/LTCG as equity. ELSS: 80C deduction up to ₹1.5L
  **old tax regime only**, 3-year lock-in.

## Settlement & mechanics
- Settlement: **T+1** standard since Jan 2023; optional **T+0** (same-day) live for the
  top-500 stocks (beta, being expanded in 2026). Narration: "shares reach your demat the
  next working day."
- Order types: market, limit, stop-loss (SL/SL-M); CNC vs MIS (intraday) product types.
- Circuit limits on stocks (5/10/20%) and market-wide circuit breakers (10/15/20%).

## Derivatives specifics (verified Jan 2026 values)
- **Nifty 50 lot size: 65** (revised from 75, effective Jan 2026 series). Bank Nifty 30,
  FinNifty 60, Midcap Select 120. Source: NSE circular / Kotak / Angel One Jan 2026.
  (In narration, prefer "around 65" + "lot sizes change from time to time".)
- Weekly index expiries: **Nifty = Tuesday** (since 1 Sep 2025), **Sensex = Thursday**;
  monthly = last such weekday. One weekly-expiry index per exchange (SEBI curbs).
- SEBI F&O curbs (2024–25): fewer weekly expiries, bigger contract sizes (~₹15–20 lakh),
  upfront premium collection, higher expiry-day margins — cite as "SEBI has been
  tightening F&O rules to protect retail traders".
- F&O margin: futures need SPAN+exposure margin (roughly 10–15% of contract value —
  present as "a fraction upfront, hence leverage"); option BUYER pays only premium;
  option SELLER posts large margin, unlimited-loss profile.

## Mutual funds / ETF facts (stable)
- NAV, expense ratio (TER; SEBI caps ~2.25% max for equity regular; direct plans cheaper
  by the distributor commission), direct vs regular, growth vs IDCW.
- Categories: large/mid/small-cap, flexi-cap, index funds, ELSS, debt, hybrid, liquid.
- ETFs trade on the exchange like a share, need demat; index funds bought via AMC/MF
  platforms without demat; tracking error concept. India's first ETF: Nifty BeES (2001).
- SIP minimums: many AMCs allow ₹500; SEBI has pushed small-ticket ₹250 SIPs ("chhoti SIP",
  2025) — say "SIPs start as small as ₹250–500".
- Rupee-cost averaging + compounding math: use computed examples (e.g. ₹5,000/month at
  12% for 20 years ≈ ₹49.96 lakh corpus on ₹12 lakh invested — computed via annuity
  formula in-scene, not quoted).
