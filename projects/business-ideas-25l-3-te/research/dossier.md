# Business Ideas Under ₹25 Lakh — VOLUME 3 (the common-man ideas) — Dossier (verified 2026-08-01)

Third video in the series. V1 = popular ideas; V2 = emerging/tailwind ideas. **V3 =
very practical "common-man" businesses anyone can start**, plus a couple of slightly
advanced/higher-capex ones for those with more money or skill. The point of V3: a
person with a small budget, in a small town or village, should look at this and think
"I can actually do this one."

All figures are **indicative industry ranges** cross-checked across 2025–26 sources.
Real cost varies by **city, scale, vendor, and owned vs rented space**. Shown as
RANGES, never promises. **Educational only — NOT financial / investment advice.**

Audience: first-time founder in AP/TS, budget **under ₹25 lakh**. Screen + narration:
Telugu + English (Tanglish); brands, ₹, %, numbers stay Latin.

Reused framework (5 lenses): పెట్టుబడి · గ్రాస్ మార్జిన్ · పేబ్యాక్ · వర్కింగ్ క్యాపిటల్ · మోట్ &
రిస్క్ + temperament. Common setup: Udyam (free), GST, FSSAI (any food), Shops &
Establishment, MUDRA (≤₹10L collateral-free), PMEGP (15–35% subsidy).

Legend: 🟢 easy/low · 🟡 medium · 🔴 hard/high. Icons: 👨‍👩‍👧 common-man · 🎓 needs skill · ⚙️ advanced.

## The 10 ideas (Volume 3)

### 1. Tiffin / dabba home-food service (టిఫిన్ · భోజనం సర్వీస్) — food, 👨‍👩‍👧 common-man 🍱
- **Capex ₹0.3–2 L**: kitchen upgrade, tiffin boxes/containers, extra gas stove,
  first groceries, delivery bag. **Runs from home.**
- **Economics**: charge **₹70–120/meal**; margin **30–50%** (~₹25–40 profit/tiffin);
  monthly net **₹25k–1L+** as subscribers grow. **Payback ~90 days — very fast.**
- **Licences**: **FSSAI (home-food registration)**, Udyam.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 daily consistency, delivery, taste; win on **subscription** + a niche
  (office-goers, students, diet/veg). CAGR 20–25%.

### 2. Tailoring & boutique (టైలరింగ్ · బొటిక్) — service, 🎓 common-man ✂️
- **Capex ₹1–8 L**: sewing + embroidery machines, materials, shop deposit (home ₹1.5L →
  boutique with staff up to ₹8L).
- **Economics**: alteration margin **40–60%**, boutique **30–60%**; monthly net
  **₹20k–1.2L** (skilled niche tailors more).
- **Licences**: Udyam, Shops & Establishment, GST (on threshold).
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 skill + design + **seasonal** (festival/wedding); win on fit, speed,
  repeat customers, and a boutique brand.

### 3. Chai / tea stall (or low-cost franchise) (చాయ్ · టీ స్టాల్) — food, 👨‍👩‍👧 common-man ☕
- **Capex ₹0.3–5 L**: roadside stall ₹0.3–1L; tea café ₹3–7L; low-cost franchise from
  ₹1.5L (Maa Ki Chai / Chai Sutta style, some **0% royalty**).
- **Economics**: margin **30–50%/cup**; daily profit **₹1k–5k** (stall) up to ₹20k
  (café). **Break-even 4–9 months.**
- **Licences**: FSSAI, local vending/trade licence, GST.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 **location + footfall** + consistency; win on spot, snacks add-on,
  and a recognisable brand/franchise.

### 4. Beauty parlour / salon (బ్యూటీ పార్లర్ · సెలూన్) — service, 🎓 common-man 💇
- **Capex ₹0.4–15 L**: home parlour ₹40–50k; neighbourhood ₹1–3L; full salon with staff
  ₹6–15L.
- **Economics**: home **50–70%** margin, walk-in **25–45%**; a small salon (8–10 clients/
  day @ ₹600) does **₹1.5–2L/month revenue**, net 20–30%.
- **Licences**: Udyam, Shops & Establishment, trade, GST.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 skill + repeat clients + location; win on service quality, packages,
  bridal/party makeup. Wellness market growing 10–15%/yr.

### 5. Mobile repair & accessories (మొబైల్ రిపేర్ · యాక్సెసరీస్) — service/retail, 🎓 common-man 📱
- **Capex ₹0.8–4 L**: repair tools + spares ₹0.8–1.5L; accessories stock ₹0.5–1L;
  combined shop ₹3–10L.
- **Economics**: repair = high margin; **accessories 20–60%**; phones 10–30%; **payback
  12–18 months**.
- **Licences**: Udyam, GST, Shops & Establishment.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 needs **repair training**, fast-changing tech; win on repair skill +
  high-margin accessories + trust. "Repair over replace" tailwind.

### 6. Goat / sheep farming (మేకలు · గొర్రెల పెంపకం) — agri, 👨‍👩‍👧 rural common-man 🐐
- **Capex ₹3–10 L**: 40–50 goats + shed + feed (~1 acre). Small start ₹1–2L for 10–20.
- **Economics**: **₹2,500–4,000 profit/goat/year**; a 50-goat farm → **₹1.25–2L+/year**,
  ROI **20–40%**; profit in **12–18 months**; twins/triplets grow the herd.
- **Licences**: **National Livestock Mission subsidy up to 50%**, local animal husbandry.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 disease/mortality, feed cost, price swings; de-risk with breed choice,
  vaccination, and a fixed mandi/festival buyer.

### 7. Pickle / papad home unit (పచ్చడి · అప్పడాలు) — food processing, 👨‍👩‍👧 common-man / women 🥒
- **Capex ₹0.3–2 L**: kitchen, drying racks, sealing/packaging.
- **Economics**: pickle margin **>50%** (200g jar costs ₹30–40, sells ₹80–120); papad
  gross **50–60%**; home unit net **₹10–35k/month**, scales with volume.
- **Licences**: **FSSAI (home-food category)**, Udyam.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 shelf life, hygiene, consistency; win on a **hero recipe** + local +
  online + festival demand. Ideal women-led home business.

### 8. CSC / Digital Seva + Xerox-stationery (CSC · జిరాక్స్ · ఆన్‌లైన్ సేవలు) — service, 👨‍👩‍👧 common-man 🖨️
- **Capex ₹1–3 L**: computer, printer/scanner, biometric device, internet, small shop,
  stationery stock.
- **Economics**: commissions (Aadhaar, PAN, bill pay, banking BC, insurance) + Xerox/
  print/lamination/photo + stationery retail; monthly net **₹15k–50k**.
- **Licences**: **CSC VLE registration (TEC certificate)**, Udyam, GST.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 depends on footfall + range of services; win in a **town/village with
  no competitor**, add printing, lamination, passport photos, online-form filling.

### 9. CCTV + solar installation service (CCTV · సోలార్ ఇన్‌స్టాలేషన్) — service, ⚙️ advanced 📹
- **Capex ₹2–8 L**: tools, ladder/vehicle, DVR + camera stock, meter, technician training.
- **Economics**: install **₹800–2,500/camera**; a job ₹25k–2.5L; service margin **25–40%**;
  a 3 kW rooftop-solar project ≈ ₹1.6–1.8L revenue, gross **15–25%**.
- **Licences**: Udyam, GST; for solar — state DISCOM empanelment / vendor tie-up.
- **Moat/risk**: 🟡 skill + steady project flow; win on **residential/B2B AMC** contracts
  + ride the solar-subsidy (PM Surya Ghar) wave. CCTV market ~20% CAGR.

### 10. Gym / fitness studio (జిమ్ · ఫిట్‌నెస్ స్టూడియో) — service, ⚙️ advanced (higher capex) 🏋️
- **Capex ₹8–25 L**: equipment (basic ₹5–15L), flooring, mirrors, AC, deposit — a
  **neighbourhood gym / boutique studio** fits under 25L (a full branded gym costs more).
- **Economics**: membership **₹1,000–3,000/month**; 200 members @ ₹2,000 = ₹4L revenue,
  net **₹50k–1L**; margin **30–40%**; **break-even 18–24 months**.
- **Licences**: Udyam, Shops & Establishment, trade, GST, fire/safety.
- **Moat/risk**: 🔴 high capex + **retention** + location; win on personal-training /
  nutrition upsell, community, and a specialisation. Fitness market doubling by 2030.

## 90-day start plan (any idea)
1. Days 1–15 — pick by 5 lenses + temperament; talk to 20 buyers; 1-page numbers plan.
2. Days 16–30 — Udyam + bank + GST; site/space; vendor quotes; check subsidy (NLM/PMEGP/MUDRA).
3. Days 31–60 — arrange money; buy machine/stock/animals; FSSAI/licences; **skill training**.
4. Days 61–90 — trial run; first customers; keep 3–6 months working-capital cushion.

## Common mistakes (kill list)
- Picking a capex too big for your cash/skill instead of the simple one that fits.
- No training on skill-based ones (tailoring, parlour, mobile repair, gym).
- All money on setup, nothing for working capital / marketing.
- No repeat/subscription plan (tiffin, parlour, gym live on repeat customers).
- Ignoring location for footfall businesses (chai, CSC, gym, parlour).
- Skipping FSSAI/licences on food ideas → fines, shutdown.
