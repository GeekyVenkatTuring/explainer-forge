Revenue/PAT from results-day coverage (Business Standard / Equitybulls / company PR).

Education only, not advice.

Fmt: TICKER | price | P/E | Q result | moat

1. **Maruti Suzuki (MARUTI)** | ₹14,037 | P/E 30.8
   - Q1 FY27 (standalone): Revenue ₹52,456 cr (+37% YoY), Net profit ₹3,352 cr (−11% YoY);
     EBITDA margin 8.22% vs 10.4% (raw-material + mix pressure). Volumes +29% (SMG Kharkhoda ramp),
     domestic share 41.2%. [3 sources agree on 3,352/52,456; one outlier 3,447]
   - Business: India's #1 PV maker, Suzuki small-car engineering, widest range small car→SUV.
   - MOAT: unmatched ~4,000-outlet sales+service network (esp. rural), scale/cost leadership,
     Suzuki small-car/hybrid tech, trusted resale value. Widest distribution = structural edge.

2. **Hyundai Motor India (HYUNDAI)** | ₹2,200 | P/E 36.1
   - Q1 FY27 (consol): Revenue ₹16,335 cr (~flat), Net profit ₹888.62 cr (−35% YoY) — hit by
     Chennai plant fire + West Asia export disruption. Rural mix all-time high 26%. Final div ₹21.
   - Business: India PV #2, SUV-heavy (Creta/Venue), major exporter from India.
   - MOAT: premium brand pull, SUV design/feature leadership, large export base (de-risks India),
     strong dealer network. Brand + product-design premium.

3. **Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles (TMPV)** | ₹347 | P/E ~1.5 (NOT MEANINGFUL — demerger/JLR one-offs; verify)
   - Q1 FY27 due 13-Aug (not out). Latest reported (Q4 FY26): Revenue ₹1,05,447 cr, Net profit ₹5,878 cr (JLR-inclusive).
   - Q1 FY27 India PV sales +46% YoY (182,574 units); EV volumes +112% (34,467).
   - Business: post-demerger Tata PV + EV + JLR (Jaguar Land Rover). Domestic EV-car leader.
   - MOAT: India EV-car first-mover + charging ecosystem, Nexon/Punch brand strength; JLR = global
     luxury brand + tech. Note: JLR cyclicality is the swing factor.

4. **Mahindra & Mahindra (M&M)** | ₹3,502 | P/E 22.7
   - Q1 FY27: Consol net profit ₹5,455 cr (+33.6% YoY), Revenue ₹57,533 cr (+26.6%);
     Standalone PAT ₹3,685 cr (+7%), Rev ₹41,920 cr (+23%). Broad-based auto+farm+services.
   - Business: SUV leader (Thar/Scorpio/XUV/BE EVs) + India #1 tractors.
   - MOAT: tractor-market leadership (distribution + brand in rural India), hot SUV franchise,
     farm+auto dual engine. Rural reach + tractor dominance = durable moat.

5. **Force Motors (FORCEMOT)** | ₹18,500 | P/E 22.2
   - Q1 FY27 (consol): Net profit ₹216.59 cr (+22.8% YoY), Revenue ₹2,440 cr (+6.2%).
   - Business: LCV/MUV (Traveller/Urbania/Gurkha), tractors; assembles engines/axles for BMW & Mercedes-Benz India.
   - MOAT: near-monopoly in shared-mobility vans (Traveller), captive engine-assembly contracts with
     BMW/Merc (sticky, high-trust), Firodia engineering. Niche dominance.

6. **Hero MotoCorp (HEROMOTOCO)** | ₹5,725 | P/E 20.7
   - Q1 FY27 (consol): Net profit ₹1,418 cr (−17% YoY, high base w/ prior one-off), Revenue ₹13,126 cr (+35%).
   - Business: world's #1 2W maker by volume; commuter Splendor/HF Deluxe; Vida EV, premium push.
   - MOAT: unrivalled scale + rural distribution (largest 2W network), commuter brand trust,
     lowest cost/unit. Distribution + scale; watch EV/premium transition.

7. **Bajaj Auto (BAJAJ-AUTO)** | ₹11,662 | P/E 27.2
   - Q1 FY27 (standalone): Revenue ₹17,244 cr (+37%), PAT ₹2,983 cr (+42%). Consol PAT ₹3,226 cr (+46%);
     consol revenue distorted by BAIHAG (KTM holding) consolidation from Nov-2025.
   - Business: 2W (Pulsar/Chetak EV) + dominant 3W/quadricycle; heavy exporter; KTM/Husqvarna stakes.
   - MOAT: #1 global-facing export franchise, 3W near-duopoly, premium-motorcycle brand + KTM tech,
     high margins/ROCE. Export + 3W + premium = wide moat.

8. **TVS Motor (TVSMOTOR)** | ₹4,441 | P/E 61.0
   - Q1 FY27 (consol): Net profit ₹1,057.6 cr (+64.5%), Revenue ₹16,296 cr (+33.5%). E-2W +86% (129,940 units).
   - Business: 2W/3W (Apache/Jupiter/iQube EV); Norton, strong exports.
   - MOAT: premiumization + design, fast-growing EV (iQube) leadership, brand + export mix.
     Execution + EV momentum (rich P/E prices this in).

9. **Eicher Motors (EICHERMOT)** | ₹8,020 | P/E 37.8
   - Q1 FY27 (consol): Net profit ₹1,463 cr (+21%), Revenue ₹6,632 cr (+31.5%). RE record 332,940 units (+27%).
     Board OK'd ₹1,225 cr AP greenfield plant.
   - Business: Royal Enfield (mid-size 250–750cc near-monopoly) + 50% VECV (Volvo trucks JV).
   - MOAT: Royal Enfield brand = category-defining moat (aspirational, ~85% mid-size share),
     pricing power, cult loyalty + expanding exports. One of the strongest brand moats in Indian autos.

10. **Atul Auto (ATULAUTO)** | ₹577 | P/E 33.2
    - Q1 FY27: Net profit ₹7.93 cr (+169% YoY off low base), Revenue ₹214.74 cr (+42%). EBITDA margin 7.72%.
    - Business: Gujarat 3W maker (Atul Gem/Shakti), diesel/CNG/petrol/electric last-mile.
    - MOAT: modest — regional 3W brand, dealer relationships, EV 3W push. Small player vs Bajaj/Piaggio;
      moat is niche + turnaround, not structural.

11. **Ather Energy (ATHERENERG)** | ₹1,482 | P/E N/A (loss-making; FY26 net loss ₹517 cr)
    - Q1 FY27: Revenue ₹1,217 cr (+89%), Net loss narrowed to ₹51 cr (from ₹178 cr); EBITDA turned POSITIVE.
      Wholesales 83,173 (+81%); pre-orders +158%.
    - Business: premium e-scooter (450 series), in-house Atherstack software + Ather Grid fast-charging.
    - MOAT: tech/software differentiation, owned fast-charging network, premium brand + vertical integration.
      Emerging moat (software + charging); still loss-making.

12. **Ola Electric (OLAELEC)** | ₹41.1 | P/E N/A (loss-making)
    - Q1 FY27: Net loss ₹336 cr (narrowed from ₹428 cr), Revenue ₹455 cr (−45% YoY); registrations −25%.
      Raised ₹780 cr QIP. Promoter holding down to 32.96%.
    - Business: e-2W (S1 series), vertically integrated incl. own cell gigafactory ambition; entering e-motorcycles.
    - MOAT: WEAK/contested — scale + vertical integration in theory, but losing share, cash-burn, trust issues.
      Bet on execution, not a proven moat.

13. **Olectra Greentech (OLECTRA)** | ₹1,385 | P/E 64.0
    - Q1 FY27 due 13-Aug (not out). Latest (Q1 FY26): PAT ₹26 cr (+8.5%), Rev ₹347 cr (+10.6%).
    - Business: India's #1 e-bus maker (BYD tech collaboration) + composite/silicone insulators; MEIL group.
    - MOAT: e-bus market leadership + BYD platform + MEIL parent (order access), STU order pipeline.
      First-mover in e-bus; execution/working-capital risk. (Rich P/E on order-book hopes.)

14. **Mercury EV-Tech (MERCURYEV)** | ₹35.0 | P/E 159 (very high; tiny earnings)
    - Q1 FY27 due 13-Aug (not out). Latest sales Q4 FY26 ₹25.29 cr; Q1 FY27 sales ~₹20.21 cr (declining). ROE 1.5%.
    - Business: micro-cap EV — e-2W/3W, e-rickshaws, EV components, battery tech.
    - MOAT: essentially none — sub-scale, weak returns, earnings lean on other income. Speculative micro-cap.

15. **Swaraj Engines (SWARAJENG)** | ₹3,600 | P/E 21.4
    - Q1 FY27 (standalone): Net profit ₹55.53 cr (+11%), Revenue ₹588.39 cr (+21.5%). Engine sales 56,803 (+15.8%).
    - Business: diesel engines for Swaraj-brand tractors (Mahindra). Cumulative >2M engines.
    - MOAT: captive, sole-supplier relationship to Mahindra's Swaraj tractors = highly sticky, high-ROCE,
      cash-rich. Narrow but very deep captive moat (also its dependency risk).

16. **VST Tillers Tractors (VSTTILLERS)** | ₹4,487 | P/E 37.1
    - Q1 FY27 due 13-Aug (not out). Q1 volumes +22.5% (power tillers strong).
    - Business: India's leading power-tiller maker + small tractors/weeders; small-farm mechanisation.
    - MOAT: dominant power-tiller share (category leader), dealer + service reach in small-farm belt,
      Japanese-tech lineage. Category leadership in a niche.

17. **Tube Investments (TIINDIA)** | ₹2,772 | P/E 81.2
    - Q1 FY27 due 14-Aug (not out). Latest (Q1 FY26): consol PAT ~₹303 cr.
    - Business: Murugappa engineering — precision steel tubes, bicycles (Hercules/BSA), chains, metal-formed;
      TI Clean Mobility (e-3W/tractors/trucks).
    - MOAT: Murugappa capital-allocation + diversified engineering platform, tube/chain leadership,
      optionality in EV. Conglomerate quality + reinvestment engine (high P/E prices growth optionality).

18. **Atlas Cycles (ATLASCYCLE)** | ₹99.7 | P/E N/A (loss-making; FY26 loss ₹8 cr, sales ₹6.9 cr)
    - Business: once India's largest bicycle brand; cycle operations largely dormant; legacy real-estate assets.
    - MOAT: none operationally today — value story is legacy land/assets (P/B 0.17x), not the business.
