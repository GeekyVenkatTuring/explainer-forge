TYRE MAJORS
1. **MRF (MRF)** | ₹1,34,190 | P/E 23.0
   - Q1 FY27 (Jun-26): Sales ₹7,676 cr, Net profit ₹500 cr. (FY26 PAT ₹2,355 cr, +29%.)
   - India's largest & priciest tyre stock; every category from 2W to aircraft, plus paints/toys/belts.
   - MOAT: WIDE — flagship brand, scale, disciplined pricing, leadership in premium replacement. Priciest share on the exchange.

2. **Apollo Tyres (APOLLOTYRE)** | ₹445 | P/E 13.2
   - Q1 FY27: Revenue ₹7,398 cr (+12.8%), Net profit ₹349 cr (multi-fold jump vs ₹12.88 cr low base). CFO resigned.
   - Passenger/CV/2W/farm tyres; India + Europe (Vredestein premium brand).
   - MOAT: WIDE — strong Indian brand + European premium (Vredestein), broad OEM+replacement, pan-Europe plants. Cheapest P/E of the majors.

3. **Balkrishna Industries — BKT (BALKRISIND)** | ₹2,466 | P/E 33.9
   - Q1 FY27: Revenue ₹3,455 cr (+25%), Net profit ₹451 cr (+56%). EBITDA margin 21.5%. 200% interim div.
   - Off-highway tyres (agri/mining/construction), largely export replacement (Europe/Americas).
   - MOAT: WIDE — low-cost specialist in a niche global OHT market (~6% world share), high margins, deep export moat.

4. **CEAT (CEATLTD)** | ₹3,723 | P/E 23.7
   - Q1 FY27: Revenue ₹4,318 cr (+22%), Net profit ₹4 cr (−96%!) — West-Asia commodity inflation crushed margins.
   - RPG-group; strong 2W and truck-bus tyres, OEM + replacement + exports.
   - MOAT: NARROW — good 2W/TBB replacement brand, but a price-taker on rubber/crude; margins swing hard.

5. **JK Tyre (JKTYRE)** | ₹395 | P/E 15.8
   - Q1 FY27: Revenue ₹3,946 cr (~flat), Net profit ₹44 cr (−73%, raw-material inflation). Domestic volume +25%.
   - Radial pioneer; trucks/buses, cars, off-highway; plants in India + Mexico.
   - MOAT: NARROW — truck-radial (TBR) strength + scale, but leveraged and cyclical.

6. **Goodyear India (GOODYEAR)** | ₹803 | P/E 24.3
   - Q1 FY27: Revenue ₹616 cr, Net profit ₹9.69 cr.
   - US-parent arm; farm/tractor + passenger tyres.
   - MOAT: NARROW — global brand + farm-tyre niche, but small and licensing-linked to the US parent.

7. **TVS Srichakra (TVSSRICHAK)** | ₹3,965 | P/E 43.5
   - Q1 FY27: Revenue ₹819 cr, Net profit ₹13 cr.
   - TVS-group; 2W/3W tyres (TVS Eurogrip) + off-highway/specialty.
   - MOAT: NARROW — focused 2W-tyre brand within the TVS ecosystem; margin-sensitive to rubber.

RUBBER / RECYCLING / LEGACY
8. **Tinna Rubber & Infrastructure (TINNARUBR)** | ₹1,108 | P/E 32.4
   - Q1 FY27: Revenue ₹156 cr, Net profit ₹21 cr.
   - Recycled rubber & crumb from end-of-life tyres; crumb-rubber-modified bitumen (CRMB), reclaim rubber.
   - MOAT: EMERGING — leader in a growing tyre-recycling/sustainability niche; first-mover scale, ESG tailwind.

9. **Elgi Rubber (ELGIRUBCO)** | ₹63.0 | P/E N/A (loss)
   - Latest (Jun-25): Sales ₹86 cr, Net loss ~₹2 cr.
   - Tyre-retreading materials, rubber machinery, reclaimed rubber; global retreading value chain.
   - MOAT: WEAK — niche retreading/recycling, but sub-scale and periodically loss-making.

10. **Tolins Tyres (TOLINS)** | ₹102 | P/E 26.8
    - Latest (Mar-26): Sales ₹45.57 cr, Net profit ₹2.67 cr.
    - Kerala-based; tyres, tubes, precured tread rubber, compounds.
    - MOAT: WEAK — small regional player across tyre + retread value chain; limited scale/edge.

11. **Modi Rubber (MODIRUBBER)** | ₹126 | P/E 493 (n.m.)
    - Latest (Mar-26): Sales ₹12 cr, Net loss ₹14 cr.
    - Legacy tyre brand; core tyre ops shrunk, now investment/asset interests.
    - MOAT: NONE — value case (if any) is legacy assets/investments, not the tyre business.

12. **PTL Enterprises (PTL)** | ₹39.8 | P/E 11.6
    - Q1 FY27: Net profit ₹8.75 cr (board 6-Aug). Earns lease income on the Kerala plant leased to Apollo Tyres; Artemis healthcare interests.
    - MOAT: NARROW — stable annuity-style lease income (low risk), but a holding-type entity tied to the Apollo promoter group.

BATTERIES / ENERGY (the duopoly)
13. **Exide Industries (EXIDEIND)** | ₹490 | P/E 44.4
    - Q1 FY27 (consol): Revenue ₹5,528 cr (+17.6%), Net profit ₹351 cr (+28%). Standalone PAT ₹407 cr. EBITDA margin 12.4%.
    - India's largest lead-acid battery maker (auto + industrial + inverters); building a lithium-ion cell gigafactory; life-insurance arm.
    - MOAT: WIDE — #1 in a battery duopoly, brand + nationwide distribution + replacement demand; lithium optionality (heavy capex).

14. **Amara Raja Energy & Mobility (ARE&M)** | ₹932 | P/E 26.6
    - Q1 FY27 due ~11-Aug (pending). Latest (Q4 FY26): Sales ₹3,460 cr, Net profit ₹322 cr (+93% YoY).
    - Amaron/PowerZone lead-acid batteries; lithium-ion cell plant, chargers, EV packs (new-energy push).
    - MOAT: WIDE — the #2 in the lead-acid duopoly with Exide; strong Amaron brand + distribution; new-energy optionality.
