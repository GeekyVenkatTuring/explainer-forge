## 1. Live-commerce / livestream selling (直播带货)
- China e-commerce livestreaming GMV > ¥4.9 trillion (2023); ~31.9% of total e-commerce GMV
  (up from 17.9% in 2021). Douyin (TikTok China, 750M+ DAU) + Taobao Live lead.
  Source: Statista / ECDB (livestream-commerce in China), 2025.
- Why it exploded: entertainment + instant discounts + trust of a host; algorithmic feed.
- India adaptation: creators/sellers doing live shopping on Instagram, YouTube, Meesho, Flipkart
  live, plus small brands running their own live sessions. Low capex — phone, ring light, samples.
  INDICATIVE: setup ₹2–8L (inventory + studio corner + phone rig); net margin 15–30% on curated
  products; payback fast (weeks–months) if you own inventory sourcing.

## 2. Community group-buying (社区团购)
- Originated 2018 (Xingsheng Youxuan, Nice Tuan); Meituan + Pinduoduo scaled it 2020 via subsidies.
  Consolidated 2025 — Meituan closed Youxuan; Pinduoduo Duoduo Maicai remains. WeChat mini-program
  "团长" (group leader) per community pools bulk grocery orders at a discount, next-day pickup.
  Source: KrAsia, Pandaily, DealStreetAsia, 2025.
- India adaptation: apartment/colony-level group buying of fruits, veg, staples via WhatsApp +
  a "society captain"; direct from mandi/FPO. INDICATIVE: setup ₹3–10L (a small hub + van +
  working capital); thin gross 12–20%, volume game; hyperlocal density is the moat.

## 3. C2M factory-direct D2C (Pinduoduo model)
- C2M = consumer demand aggregated → sent direct to factory → no middlemen → 30–40% cheaper.
  Pinduoduo built brands from OEM factories; guochao D2C brands ride the same rails.
  Source: Pandaily, Daxue Consulting (C2M in China), FourWeekMBA 2026.
- India adaptation: a founder partners with a cluster factory (Tiruppur textiles, Moradabad,
  Agra footwear, Rajkot) and sells direct online under one brand at factory-plus pricing.
  INDICATIVE: setup ₹5–20L (brand, first inventory, packaging, ads); net 15–25%; moat = a hero
  SKU + reviews + repeat.

## 4. Value F&B franchise chains (Mixue / Luckin model)
- Mixue: 53,000+ stores globally (> McDonald's), 99%+ franchised; ~₹1 items; franchisee invest
  ~¥210,000 (~$29,000). Revenue mostly from supplying ingredients/equipment to franchisees.
  HK IPO Mar 2025 oversubscribed ~5,000×. Luckin: 22,300+ stores end-2024, overtook Starbucks
  in China; cashier-less app-only, 30–50% coupon discounting.
  Source: CKGSB, Britannica Money, TIME100 2025, NRN, CNBC 2025-26.
- Why it exploded: ultra-low price + in-house supply chain + dense franchising.
- India adaptation: a value ice-cream/tea/coffee kiosk chain — low ticket (₹20–60), own supply,
  franchise out. INDICATIVE per outlet ₹8–20L; net 12–20%; brand + supply chain is the moat.

## 5. Pre-made meals / RTE (预制菜) + central kitchen
- China prepared-dishes market ~¥630B by 2025 (from ¥244.5B in 2019, ¥420B in 2022); central
  kitchens industrializing. Source: Statista, IndexBox, Mintel 2025.
- India adaptation: central kitchen making ready-to-cook/ready-to-eat regional meals (biryani kits,
  curries, batters) for retail + cloud kitchens + QSR supply. INDICATIVE ₹20–45L (kitchen, blast
  freezer, packaging, FSSAI); net 10–18%; cold chain + shelf life are the constraints.

## 6. EV services — charging / battery-swap / e-2/3-wheeler
- NIO 3,399 swap stations, 550 cities (Jul 2025). Battery-swap for e-2W market $0.46B (2025) →
  $1.25B (2031), 18% CAGR; two/three-wheelers + fleets lead (high utilization = swap wins).
  Source: MarketsandMarkets, Mordor, JATO 2025.
- India adaptation: battery-swap points + fast-charging for e-rickshaws/2W delivery fleets; or an
  e-2/3W dealership + service. INDICATIVE ₹15–40L (batteries/chargers/site or showroom+service);
  service/energy is the recurring moat, thin hardware margin.

## 7. Pet economy
- China pet sector > ¥811.4B ($114B) in 2025, growing ~3× global peers; urban dog+cat spend
  ¥312.6B (+4.1%); pet food 53.7% of spend; grooming products ~$895M (2025) → $1.86B (2033), 9.5%
  CAGR. Source: China Daily, Xinhua, Grandview, CIPS 2025.
- India adaptation: pet grooming + boarding + food/accessories retail; house-call grooming.
  INDICATIVE ₹8–25L (studio, van, inventory); net 15–30% services; recurring + trust moat.

## 8. Silver economy — elderly products/care
- China 60+ = 320M (23% of population) end-2025; silver economy ~¥7T (2024) → ~¥30T by 2035;
  household medical devices market > ¥380B by 2025. Source: Xinhua, HKTDC, China-Briefing 2025.
- India adaptation: elder-care services (home attendants, physiotherapy, day-care), assistive
  products, senior-friendly retail. INDICATIVE ₹10–30L (staff, training, equipment); net 15–25%;
  trust + reliability moat; India ageing wave is ~10 yrs behind China (early-mover window).

## 9. Guochao national-pride D2C branding
- ~80% of China Gen Z engage with guochao; Li-Ning/Anta strong; Songmont handbags +90% online
  (2025) while Gucci −50%. "Guochao 3.0" = cultural pride + genuinely better product.
  Source: Daxue, CKGSB, Xinhua 2026.
- India adaptation: "Made-in-India / vocal-for-local" heritage D2C — khadi-modern, ayurveda,
  regional crafts, desi-pride apparel/FMCG. INDICATIVE ₹5–20L (brand, inventory, content, ads);
  net 15–30%; story + community is the moat.

## 10. Unmanned / self-service retail (smart vending / stores)
- China retail vending ~$4.79B (2024) → $6.57B (2030); unmanned convenience-store segment $3.93B
  (2025) → $38.21B (2035), ~25.5% CAGR; smart cabinets (face/QR scan) replacing old machines;
  ~2M smart vending units target 2025. Source: Grandview, MRFuture, Asia Vending Expo 2025.
- India adaptation: smart vending / self-service micro-stores in offices, gyms, metro, hostels,
  hospitals (UPI-paid). INDICATIVE ₹6–20L (2–6 machines + stock + software); net 20–35% per
  location; location contracts + restocking logistics are the moat.

## Series framing (intro + recap)
- Why China → India: China is ~10–15 yrs ahead on consumer-internet + urbanization; many models
  that matured there map onto India's rising incomes, cheap data, UPI, and young population. The
  point is NOT "copy blindly" — it's "read the pattern, adapt to Indian unit economics + regulation."
- Adaptation lens repeated per idea: (a) the China model, (b) why it exploded, (c) the India
  adaptation + indicative unit economics, (d) how to start / playbook, (e) moat & risks.
- Hard honesty: China's subsidy-fuelled versions (group-buying) burned cash; Indian founders must
  build on real unit economics from day one. Say this.
