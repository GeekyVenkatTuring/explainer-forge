## B. Setup / legal
- **IEC** (Import Export Code): 10-digit, DGFT, issued in 1–2 days, tied to PAN. ~free/nominal.
- **GST registration**: required — pay **IGST at customs**, reclaim as **Input Tax Credit**.
- Documents: IEC, Bill of Entry, commercial invoice, packing list, Bill of Lading/AWB,
  certificate of origin, BIS cert (if applicable), LMPC.

## C. Sourcing
- **Alibaba** (English, higher MOQ) · **1688** (cheaper, Chinese-only → need agent) · **Canton Fair**.
- Sourcing agent: QC, consolidation, pays supplier without a Chinese bank account.
- Vet: 5+ yrs, verified/audited badge, <24h response, 95%+ reviews. **Sample before bulk.**

## E. Import duty (order of calculation)
1. Assessable/CIF value = FOB + freight + insurance
2. **BCD** = 5–40% (product-specific) × CIF  *(toys 70%)*
3. **SWS** = 10% × BCD
4. IGST base = CIF + BCD + SWS
5. **IGST** = commonly **18%** (HSN-dependent) × IGST base
6. + **Anti-dumping duty (ADD)** on select goods (steel, chemicals, ferrite cores, etc.)
- Rule of thumb: duties+GST make a shipment **~40–60% more expensive** than sticker.
- Landed cost = product + freight + duties + insurance + overhead.
- Pricing: **Selling price = landed cost ÷ (1 − target margin)**.

## F. Compliance (the gate)
- **BIS CRS** (electronics/IT — mobiles, laptops, LED lights, chargers, power banks,
  routers, TVs, adapters…): mandatory, **per-model**, **45–60 days**, **₹50k–1.5L**
  (test ₹10k–30k/model), valid **2 yrs**. 70+ categories notified. No CRS → cannot import/sell.
- **LMPC certificate + Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules 2011**: label must show
  importer name/address, net qty, month/yr of import, **MRP incl. all taxes**, generic name,
  consumer-care, **country of origin** ("Made in ___"). Re-label before clearance if missing.
- **FSSAI** for food; product-specific QCOs expanding.

## G. Worked landed-cost example (illustrative — TWS earbuds)
- Factory (FOB): ~$4.00/unit (₹340 @ ₹85/$) · MOQ 1,000
- Sea freight+insurance allocated: ~₹40/unit
- CIF ≈ ₹380 · BCD 20% = ₹76 · SWS 10%×BCD = ₹7.6 · IGST 18%×(380+76+7.6=463.6)=₹83.4
- **Landed ≈ ₹547/unit** (before BIS amortization + branding/packaging + marketing)
- + BIS CRS amortized (₹1L / 5,000 units = ₹20) + packaging ₹30 + returns/ops ₹50 ≈ **₹647**
- Sell ₹1,299 (D2C) → gross margin ≈ 50%; after ads/marketplace fees, net thinner.
- (Numbers illustrative; duty rate by exact HSN, forex, and MOQ change everything.)

## H. The closing windows (honesty backbone)
- **Toys**: BCD **20%→60% (Feb 2020)→70% (Mar 2023)** + mandatory **BIS QCO 2025**.
  Chinese toy imports **$235M (FY20) → ~$40M (FY25), −83%**. Pure arbitrage there is dead.
- **Anti-dumping**: >70% of India's 2024 ADD investigations targeted Chinese goods;
  new ADD on ferrite cores (used in chargers/EV/telecom), electrical steel ($223.82/ton, 5 yrs).
- **PLI / ECMS**: Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme (~$2.7B, 28 Mar 2025) —
  push domestic value-add **18–20% → 30–35%** over 4 yrs. Policy = import-substitution.
- Everyday risks: forex, MOQ cash lock-up, quality variance, DRI seizures of non-compliant
  goods, near-zero moat for pure trading, copycats.

## I. Brand proof (the durable path)
- **boAt** (2016, ~₹30L start): 0% India-made pre-Covid → **70% by 2023**; 50:50 JV with Dixon (2022).
- **Atomberg** (BLDC fans): imports magnets/electronics from China; **₹1,000 Cr** revenue in yr 10.
- **Portronics** (Delhi 2010): India design/brand, China-sourced, India-assembled.
- **Lava**: assembles phones in India, imports chipset/display/camera.
- Thesis: **source from China, sell a BRAND (design + QC + distribution), localize as you scale (China+1).**
