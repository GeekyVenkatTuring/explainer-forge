## Description
An honest, unsponsored review of INDmoney for buying US stocks from India. We answer three
questions: how the app actually works, whether your money is safe, and whether it's worth it —
including the fees nobody highlights and the complaints real users are posting.

We cover:
• How it works — sign-up + PAN KYC, adding money (₹→$ under LRS), fractional buying from $1, SIP, and withdrawal
• Is it safe? — your US shares sit at a regulated US broker (DriveWealth / Alpaca, SEC + FINRA), held in your name; SIPC covers up to $500,000 (incl. $250,000 cash) if the broker fails — NOT market losses. INDmoney's broking arm is SEBI-registered (INDstocks)
• What it really costs — the forex markup (~0.5–1.5%), not brokerage, is the true cost; TCS only above ₹10 lakh/yr (refundable); INDprime (~₹99/mo) is optional
• Worth it? — INDmoney vs Vested vs Interactive Brokers (forex, brokerage, who each is for)
• The verdict — keep it for small, long-term, all-in-one investing; look at IBKR for large sums or active trading
• Real complaints — slow US-stock withdrawals, funds stuck mid-transfer, weak support, app/KYC bugs — kept in proportion against ~4★ app-store averages
• Four habits that matter more than the app — invest in chunks, don't over-withdraw, file Schedule FA, keep records

Key facts (verified for 2026; fees/rules change — always check current sources):
– US custody via DriveWealth / Alpaca (SEC-registered, FINRA + SIPC members); SIPC up to $500,000 (incl. $250,000 cash)
– INDmoney broking arm INDstocks: SEBI Stock-Broker INZ000305337, DP IN-DP-690-2022
– Brokerage 0.25% per trade; account / AMC / withdrawal fees ₹0; forex markup ~0.5–1.5% (bank-charged)
– TCS 20% only above ₹10 lakh/FY, refundable/adjustable in your ITR
– Fractional US stocks from $1; 10,000+ US stocks & global ETFs

platform, or stock here is a recommendation. Consult a SEBI-registered advisor / chartered accountant
for your own situation.
